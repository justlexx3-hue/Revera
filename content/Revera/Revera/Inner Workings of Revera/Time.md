## The Year
- A year has **365 days**.
- Every **fourth year** is a leap year with **366 days**.
- The year is divided into **12 months**, each containing **30 days**.
- That gives **360 ordinary calendar days**.
- The remaining **5 days** are the sacred **Days Between**, which sit outside the months.

## The Months
Each month is dedicated to one of the Twelve, following the order of the pantheon:

1. **Elaris** — Elarion, Prime Light
2. **Amaris** — Amariel, the Lifebringer
3. **Syreth** — Syrethis, the Tidebearer
4. **Lyrian** — Lyriana, the Threadkeeper
5. **Rheos** — Rheon, the Arbiter
6. **Thalos** — Thalorim, the Archivist
7. **Vareth** — Vareth, the Shaper
8. **Calendra** — Calendra, the Bondweaver
9. **Kaelos** — Kaelos, the Masked
10. **Lysan** — Lysandor, the Inspirer
11. **Eliran** — Elira, the Wayfarer
12. **Eryndral** — Eryndrel, the Shepherd.

## The Week
Revera has a **six-day week**.

Because each month has 30 days, every month contains exactly **five weeks**.

The six days are:

1. **Dawn**: beginnings, hope, planning
2. **Forge**: work, labor, creation
3. **Hearth**: family, community, markets
4. **Tide**: travel, trade, exploration
5. **Veil**: reflection, learning, worship
6. **Rest**: rest, celebration, gratitude. 

That gives the calendar a wonderfully clean rhythm: **6 days × 5 weeks × 12 months = 360 days.**

## The Days Between

After the twelfth month comes a period outside normal calendar time called **the Days Between**.

There are normally **five Days Between**:

1. **The Dawning** — celebrates Elarion and the birth of the world
2. **The Bloom** — celebrates Amariel and the gift of life
3. **The Crossing** — honors Eryndrel and those who passed during the year
4. **The Concord** — celebrates the unity of the Twelve and all creation
5. **The Renewal** — the old year ends; at sunrise, the new year begins

Every fourth year, there is a **sixth Day Between**, called **The Turning**, dedicated to Lyriana because even time is considered woven by Fate.

## The Moons

Revera has **two moons**:

- **Astera**, the Light Moon. Large, luminous, silver, and associated with guidance, hope, life, and dreams.
- **Nyr**, the Veiled Moon. Smaller and darker, associated with mystery, fate, death, and change.

When **Astera and Nyr are both full**, the people of Revera believe **the Veil grows thin and wonders walk**. 

## Dating

Dates are written as:

**Day + Month + Year**

For example:

> **18th of Thalos, Year 842 AD**

The calendar uses **AD, After the Descent**, as its historical dating system. 

So, in its simplest form:

**6 days → 5 weeks → 30 days → 12 months → 360 days → 5 Days Between → 365 days.**

And every fourth year:

**360 + 6 Days Between = 366 days.**

It's a satisfyingly tidy little clockwork system, with the **Days Between** giving the year a deliberate pause before the wheel turns again.
