**Shaper:** [[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra, the Bondweaver]] 
**Gifter:** [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion, the Prime Light]]

Halflings are small people with rounded ears, prominent noses, and notably large, hairy feet. They possess keen senses and an innate connection to the magnetic forces of the Mortal Realm, giving them a reliable internal sense of direction.

Calendra shaped the Halflings around **home, family, and community**, while Elarion gifted them **hope and good fortune**. Their communities are found throughout Revera, often gathering around strong family ties and welcoming settlements.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Luckbringer:** At the beginning of each session, everyone in your party gains **1 Hope**.
- **Internal Compass:** When your Hope Die shows a **1**, you may reroll it.
