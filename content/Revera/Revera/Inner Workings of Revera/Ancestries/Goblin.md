**Shaper:** [[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos, the Masked]]
**Gifter:** [[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim, the Archivist]]

Goblins are small people with large ears, keen hearing, and excellent vision, including in darkness. Their skin and eyes can appear in a wide variety of colors, while their ears can communicate subtle expressions and emotions.

Kaelos shaped the Goblins with **adaptability and instinct**, while Thalorim gifted them **curiosity and keen observation**. Their small size and alert senses have made them natural scouts, explorers, messengers, and survivors throughout Revera.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Surefooted:** You ignore disadvantage on **Agility Rolls**.
- **Danger Sense:** Once per rest, mark **1 Stress** to force an adversary to reroll an attack against you or an ally within Very Close range.
