**Shaper:** [[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor, the Inspirer]]
**Gifter:** [[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira, the Wayfarer]] 

Aetheris are humanoids descended from celestial beings, marked by radiant features and celestial wings. Their appearance can include glowing eyes, luminous skin, and divine markings that develop as they mature.

Lysandor shaped the Aetheris through **celestial inspiration and wonder**, while Elira gifted them the desire to **travel beyond the familiar**. In Revera, they are among the peoples most visibly touched by the heavens.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Hallowed Aura:** Once per long rest, when an ally within Close range rolls with Fear, you can change that roll to Hope.
- **Celestial Wings:** You can fly. Once per scene while flying, spend **1 Hope instead of marking an Armor Slot**.
