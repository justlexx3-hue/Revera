**Shaper:** [[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel, the Sheperd]]
**Gifter:** [[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos, the Masked]]

Infernis are humanoids with fiendish characteristics such as horns, pointed ears, sharp teeth, long fingers, and sometimes tails. Their physical appearance varies dramatically between individuals.

Eryndrel shaped the Infernis with a connection to **mortality and the realms beyond**, while Kaelos gifted them **transformation and fluidity**. Their varied forms and supernatural presence have made them one of Revera's most visually distinctive ancestries.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Fearless:** When rolling with Fear, mark **2 Stress** to change the roll to Hope.
- **Dread Visage:** Gain advantage on rolls made to **intimidate hostile creatures**.
