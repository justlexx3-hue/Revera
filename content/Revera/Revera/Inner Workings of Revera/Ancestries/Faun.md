**Shaper:** [[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel, the Lifebringer]]
**Gifter:** [[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra, the Bondweaver]]

Fauns are humanoids with distinctly caprine features, including horns, fur, cloven hooves, and powerful legs. Their appearances vary greatly, with some leaning strongly toward their animal heritage and others appearing more humanoid.

Amariel shaped the Fauns with a connection to **life and the natural world**, while Calendra gifted them **companionship and community**. They are particularly common throughout Revera's forests, grasslands, and pastoral regions.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Caprine Leap:** You can leap anywhere within **Close range** as normal movement.
- **Kick:** After a successful Melee attack, mark **1 Stress** to add **2d6 damage** and knock yourself or the target back to Very Close range.
