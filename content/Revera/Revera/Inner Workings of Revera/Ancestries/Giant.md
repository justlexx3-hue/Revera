**Shaper:** All Twelve Gods  
**Gifter:** All Twelve Gods

Giants are enormous humanoids with broad shoulders, long arms and necks, and naturally powerful physiques. They can develop between one and three eyes, though they are born without sight and develop their eyes during childhood.

The Giants are unique among Revera's peoples. Rather than being created by a single divine partnership, they were shaped and gifted by **all Twelve Gods together**, each contributing a portion of their divine essence. They are therefore the only ancestry whose origin belongs equally to the entire pantheon.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Endurance:** Gain **one additional Hit Point slot** at character creation.
- **Reach:** Treat Melee-range weapons, abilities, spells, and similar features as having **Very Close range** instead.
