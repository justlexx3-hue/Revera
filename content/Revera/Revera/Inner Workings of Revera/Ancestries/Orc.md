**Shaper:** [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon, the Arbiter]]
**Gifter:** [[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel, the Sheperd]]

Orcs are sturdy, muscular people with square features, pointed ears, and prominent boar-like tusks. Their skin and hair can appear in many colors, and their tusks continue growing throughout their lives.

Rheon shaped the Orcs with **strength, resilience, and justice**, while Eryndrel gifted them an understanding of **mortality and the importance of protecting what matters**. Their divine heritage has made strength and responsibility closely linked in many Orc traditions.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Sturdy:** While you have only **1 Hit Point remaining**, attacks against you have disadvantage.
- **Tusks:** After succeeding on a Melee attack, spend **1 Hope** to deal an additional **1d6 damage**.
