**Shaper:** [[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis, the Tidebearer]]
**Gifter:** [[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira, the Wayfarer]]

Tidekin are humanoids whose bodies combine flesh and water. Their forms may display fins, webbing, flowing water, and colors associated with oceans, rivers, and deep waters. They are naturally adapted to life beneath the surface.

Syrethis shaped the Tidekin from the **ever-moving waters of Revera**, while Elira gifted them the freedom to **follow those waters wherever they lead**. Their strongest communities are naturally found along Revera's coasts and waterways.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Amphibious:** You can **breathe and move naturally underwater**.
- **Lifespring:** Once per rest, when you have access to a small amount of water, mark **1 Stress** to clear **1 Hit Point** from yourself or an ally within Very Close range.
