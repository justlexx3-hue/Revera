**Shaper:** [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion, the Prime Light]]
**Gifter:** [[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos, the Masked]]

Drakona are powerful, scaled humanoids with thick natural armor and an elemental breath. Their bodies resemble wingless dragons, though their appearance varies considerably between individuals and lineages. Their elemental nature is often inherited, though unusual variations sometimes appear.

Elarion shaped the Drakona with the **radiance and power of the Prime Light**, while Kaelos gifted them **change and elemental transformation**. Their breath is regarded by many Revan scholars as one of the clearest examples of divine power becoming part of mortal biology.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Scales:** When Severe damage would be marked, spend 1 Stress to reduce the Hit Point loss by **1**.
- **Elemental Breath:** Choose an element and attack a target or group within **Very Close range**, dealing **d8 magic damage** with an Instinct-based attack using your Proficiency.
