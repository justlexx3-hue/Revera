**Shaper:** [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion, the Prime Light]]
**Gifter:** [[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra, the Bondweaver]]

Humans are highly adaptable people capable of thriving across a wide range of environments. Their appearances and physical builds vary greatly, and their relatively short lifespans have made them one of the most widespread peoples of Revera.

Elarion shaped humanity with the capacity for **adaptation and endurance**, while Calendra gifted them their powerful bonds of **family, friendship, and community**. Human settlements can be found throughout nearly every region of Revera.

**Ancestry Features**

- **High Stamina:** Gain **one additional Stress slot** at character creation.
- **Adaptability:** After failing a roll that uses an Experience, mark **1 Stress** to reroll it.
