**Shaper:** [[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel, the Lifebringer]]
**Gifter:** [[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel, the Sheperd]]

Fungril resemble humanoid mushrooms and possess an extraordinary variety of bodies, colors, faces, and limbs. They can range from only a few feet tall to much larger forms and may live for centuries. Many communicate through a mycelial array that allows them to exchange information across great distances.

Amariel shaped the Fungril through the cycles of **growth and renewal**, while Eryndrel gifted them their unusual connection to **death and memory**. Their existence reflects the constant movement between decay and new life, making them particularly prominent in regions where those cycles are most visible.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Fungril Network:** Make an **Instinct Roll (12)** to communicate with another Fungril through your mycelial array across any distance.
- **Death Connection:** While touching a recently deceased corpse, mark **1 Stress** to extract a memory associated with a chosen emotion or sensation.
