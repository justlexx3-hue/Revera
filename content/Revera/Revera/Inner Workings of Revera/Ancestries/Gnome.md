**Shaper:** [[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim, the Archivist]]
**Gifter:** [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth, the Shaper]] 

Gnomes are small humanoids with distinctive conical heads and an innate ability to teleport short distances. Their compact forms and nimble hands make them particularly well suited to precise work and intricate construction.

Thalorim shaped the Gnomes with **curiosity and keen perception**, while Vareth gifted them the ability to turn ideas into **physical creations and clever mechanisms**. Their workshops are often filled with tools, half-finished inventions, and things that probably shouldn't have that many moving parts.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Nimble Fingers:** When making a Finesse Roll, spend **2 Hope** to reroll your Hope Die.
- **Flicker Step:** Once per scene, teleport to a point you can see within **Far range**.
