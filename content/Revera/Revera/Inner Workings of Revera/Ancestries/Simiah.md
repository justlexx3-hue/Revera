**Shaper:** [[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim, the Archivist]]
**Gifter:** [[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor, the Inspirer]]

Simiah are primate-like people with long limbs, prehensile feet, and sometimes prehensile tails. Their bodies are naturally suited to climbing, balancing, and moving through difficult terrain.

Thalorim shaped the Simiah with **curiosity and dexterity**, while Lysandor gifted them **imagination and creativity**. Their natural agility makes them common among explorers, performers, artisans, and travelers throughout Revera.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Natural Climber:** Gain advantage on Agility Rolls involving **climbing or balancing**.
- **Nimble:** Gain a permanent **+1 Evasion** at character creation.
