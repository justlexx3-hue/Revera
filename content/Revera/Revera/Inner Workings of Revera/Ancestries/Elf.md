**Shaper:** [[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel, the Lifebringer]]
**Gifter:** [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion, the Prime Light]]

Elves are tall humanoids with pointed ears and exceptionally keen senses. They can enter a celestial trance rather than sleep, and some develop a **mystic form** as their relationship with nature or magic deepens. These changes can include natural or celestial features and may shift throughout their long lives.

Amariel shaped the Elves with their connection to **life, nature, and the changing world**, while Lyriana gifted them an awareness of **time, fate, and possibility**. Their long lives allow them to witness generations of Revera's history, while their appearance can change alongside the places and forces they devote themselves to.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Quick Reactions:** Mark **1 Stress** to gain advantage on a Reaction Roll.
- **Celestial Trance:** During a rest, enter a trance to gain **one additional downtime move**.
