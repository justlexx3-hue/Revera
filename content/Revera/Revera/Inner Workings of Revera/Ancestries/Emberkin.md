**Shaper:** [[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos, the Masked]]
**Gifter:** [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion, the Prime Light]]

Emberkin are humanoids whose bodies combine flesh and fire. Their elemental nature may appear through flaming hair, glowing eyes, smoke, radiant skin, or fire visible beneath their flesh.

Kaelos shaped them through **change, transformation, and flame**, while Elarion gifted their fire his **radiance and creative spark**. Their flames are found throughout Revera in forms ranging from gentle warmth to powerful elemental fire.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Fireproof:** You are immune to damage from **magical and mundane flame**.
- **Ignition:** Mark **1 Stress** to engulf your primary weapon in flame for the scene. It sheds bright light and adds **1d6 to its damage rolls**.
