**Shaper:** [[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis, the Tidebearer]]
**Gifter:** [[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana, the Threadkeeper]]

Ribbets are amphibious frog-like people with protruding eyes, webbed hands and feet, and powerful hind legs. They hatch from eggs in water and develop from tadpoles into land-capable adults, with lifespans of roughly a century.

Syrethis shaped the Ribbets with the **adaptability and movement of water**, while Lyriana gifted them an instinct for **possibility and unexpected paths**. Their ability to move naturally between land and water has allowed them to thrive around Revera's rivers, wetlands, and coasts.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Amphibious:** You can **breathe and move naturally underwater**.
- **Long Tongue:** Your tongue can reach objects within Close range; mark **1 Stress** to use it as a Finesse Close weapon dealing **d12 physical damage** with your Proficiency.
