**Shaper:** [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth, the Shaper]] 
**Gifter:** [[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim, the Archivist]]

Clanks are sentient beings constructed from materials such as metal, wood, and stone. Their forms vary enormously, ranging from humanoid figures to specialized constructions, and many are designed with particular purposes in mind. Their bodies can be repaired, replaced, and modified throughout their lives, though the magic sustaining their minds gradually weakens with age.

Vareth shaped the Clanks with the **craftsmanship of the physical world**, while Thalorim gifted them **curiosity and the desire to learn**. Clank workshops are particularly renowned in Cael'Vareth, where ancient traditions of construction and repair are still practiced.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Purposeful Design:** Choose an Experience connected to your original purpose and gain a permanent **+1 bonus** to it.
- **Efficient:** When taking a short rest, you may choose a **long-rest move** instead of a short-rest move.
