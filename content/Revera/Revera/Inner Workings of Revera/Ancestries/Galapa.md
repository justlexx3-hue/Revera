**Shaper:** [[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis, the Tidebearer]]  
**Gifter:** [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon, the Arbiter]]

Galapa are turtle-like people with sturdy bodies and large protective shells. Their appearances vary widely, with different shell patterns, colors, and physical traits. When threatened, they can withdraw completely into their shells.

Syrethis shaped the Galapa with the **resilience of the waters**, while Rheon gifted them **steadiness and endurance**. They are naturally suited to Revera's coasts, wetlands, rivers, and other environments where patience is often as important as strength.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Shell:** Add your **Proficiency** to your damage thresholds.
- **Retract:** Mark **1 Stress** to withdraw into your shell, gaining resistance to physical damage but becoming unable to move and taking disadvantage on action rolls.
