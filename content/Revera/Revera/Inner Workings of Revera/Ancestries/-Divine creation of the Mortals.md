# The Creation of Mortals

When the Twelve first shaped Revera, they soon discovered that creation itself was not enough. The world had light, life, seas, mountains, dreams, and the turning of fate, but it lacked those who could **experience it, shape it, and choose their own path through it**.

So the Twelve came together to create mortals.

Yet no single god could create a people that truly embodied all that Revera could become. Instead, the gods worked in pairs. One would become the **Shaper**, giving an ancestry its form, nature, and place within the world. The other became the **Gifter**, placing within them a quality drawn from their own divine essence: perhaps curiosity, courage, adaptability, community, wisdom, or a connection to the natural world. Thus, every mortal people carries the touch of **two gods**.

The Shaper gives them what they **are**.  
The Gifter gives them something of **what they might become**.

The Twelve did not always agree on what mortals should be. Nor did they seek to make every people alike. Instead, their differences became part of the design. Some were shaped from stone, some from living things, some from dreams, and some from stranger forces still. Each was given a different spark, ensuring that no single ancestry could embody the whole of creation.

There was, however, one exception.

When the Twelve sought to create a people that could carry something of **all divine aspects**, they set aside their individual roles and worked as one. Thus came the **Giants**, the only mortal ancestry shaped and gifted by all Twelve gods. And when their work was finished, the gods did something perhaps more important than creation itself:

**They gave mortals the freedom to become something the gods had never imagined.**

# The 24 Peoples of Revera

## **Clank**  
Sentient beings of crafted bodies, built from materials such as metal, wood, and stone. Their forms can be remarkably varied, from humanoid figures to strange specialized constructions. In Revera, many wonder whether the soul of a Clank is something crafted, discovered, or simply waiting inside the machine to be awakened.

## **Drakona**  
Powerful, scaled people who resemble dragons in humanoid form, possessing thick natural armor and an elemental breath. Though descended from the ancient draconic tradition, they are wingless and cannot fly naturally. In Revera, their scales are often seen as a reminder of ancient forces that existed before mortal kingdoms.

## **Dwarf**  
Short, broad, exceptionally resilient people with dense musculature, thick hair, and naturally tough skin and nails. Their bodies can even accommodate gemstones, tattoos, and piercings. Revera's dwarves fit naturally among the old stone halls and mountain settlements, where patience and craftsmanship are prized almost as highly as the finished work.

## **Elf**  
Graceful and long-lived people with an innate connection to magic and the passage of time. Their appearance and traditions can vary enormously between communities, but elves often carry themselves with a sense that a single lifetime is only one thread in something much larger. In Revera, that makes their relationship with Lyriana's tapestry particularly fascinating.

## **Faerie**  
Small, winged folk whose delicate appearance belies their remarkable agility and innate magic. Their wings allow them to move through the world in ways larger peoples cannot. In Revera, they are often associated with moonlit forests, hidden places, and the strange boundary between what is real and what might yet be possible.

## **Faun**  
Humanoids with unmistakable caprine traits, including horns, fur, and powerful legs suited to remarkable leaps. They possess an energetic, physical connection to the world around them. In Revera, they feel perfectly at home among Amariel's wild places, where a forest path is as much an invitation as a road.

## **Firbolg**  
Large, sturdy folk strongly associated with the natural world, often possessing features reminiscent of the creatures and forests around them. Their physical resilience is matched by an unusual steadiness of spirit. In Revera, many see them as people who understand that the forest is not wilderness waiting to be conquered, but a living thing with which one must learn to coexist.

## **Fungril**  
Mushroom-like people whose bodies reflect their fungal nature and their intimate relationship with decay and renewal. They possess an unusual connection to the dead and to one another. Revera gives this ancestry a particularly meaningful place in the cycle of life: where something dies, something else may already be preparing to grow.

## **Galapa**  
People with sturdy turtle-like shells and bodies built for resilience. Their shells provide natural protection, while their physicality makes them remarkably difficult to dislodge. In Revera, they carry an almost geological patience, the sort that makes a hundred years seem like a perfectly reasonable amount of time to wait.

## **Giant**  
Exceptionally large and powerful people whose physical presence is matched by remarkable endurance. They are built for strength and reach, capable of feats that would be impossible for most other ancestries. In Revera, their origin is extraordinary: **the Giants were shaped and gifted collectively by all Twelve Gods**, making them the only ancestry to bear the touch of the entire pantheon.

## **Goblin**  
Small, quick-footed people with keen instincts and an uncanny ability to stay out of harm's way. They are often underestimated by larger peoples, a mistake goblins have historically found quite useful. In Revera, their adaptability fits beautifully with the unpredictable influence of Kaelos.

## **Halfling**  
Small, adaptable folk with an instinct for survival, luck, and finding their way through trouble. Their size and unassuming nature often allow them to move through places unnoticed. In Revera, they embody the idea that a person doesn't need to be mighty to make a difference.

## **Human**  
Perhaps the most broadly adaptable of the ancestries, humans possess no single overwhelming physical advantage, but are remarkably capable of adjusting to new circumstances. Their diversity is one of their defining characteristics. In Revera, humanity's greatest strength may simply be its refusal to accept that the world has already decided what it can become.

## **Infernis**  
People marked by fiendish or infernal features, including horns and an imposing presence. Their appearance can inspire fear or suspicion, but the ancestry itself does not dictate morality or temperament. In Revera, this is especially fitting: appearance is not destiny, and Kaelos would probably be rather pleased with the lesson.

## **Katari**  
Cat-like humanoids possessing keen senses, agile bodies, and retractable claws. Their physical instincts make them natural hunters and exceptionally capable movers. Across Revera, a Katari might be equally at home stalking through a moonlit forest, navigating a crowded market, or disappearing down an alley before anyone notices.

## **Orc**  
Strong, resilient people built for physical hardship and capable of enduring punishment that would fell others. Their imposing forms often lead outsiders to assumptions about their nature. Revera's theology gives them a useful counterpoint: strength has meaning only when the person possessing it decides what that strength is for.

## **Ribbet**  
Amphibious frog-like people equally at home on land and in water. Their bodies are adapted to environments that would challenge most other ancestries. In Revera, the Ribbet's connection to Syrethis and Lyriana makes their adaptability particularly fitting: they are creatures born to move between worlds, never entirely bound to one place.

## **Simiah**  
Primate-like people known for agility, climbing ability, and nimble movement. They can navigate vertical spaces and difficult terrain with remarkable ease. In Revera, they are natural wanderers of forests, cliffs, ruins, and city rooftops alike, forever finding routes that others overlooked.

# The Six from _Hope & Fear_

## **Skykin**  
Humanoids descended from air elementals, their bodies carrying the qualities of wind and open sky. They possess an innate relationship with movement and falling, and can call upon the wind to carry themselves or others. In Revera, the Skykin feel like children of the high places, where the horizon is never an ending.

## **Tidekin**  
Humanoids descended from water elementals, combining mortal flesh with the nature of water. They can breathe and move naturally underwater and possess a restorative relationship with water. Revera's seas, rivers, and storms would make the Tidekin especially at home beneath Syrethis's ever-moving waters.

## **Earthkin**  
Humanoids descended from earth elementals, their bodies combining flesh and earth. They possess exceptional physical solidity and are extraordinarily difficult to move against their will while grounded. In Revera, they seem almost like walking fragments of the ancient world itself.

## **Emberkin**  
Humanoids descended from fire elementals, with bodies combining flesh and living flame. They are naturally immune to fire and can kindle their weapons with elemental flame. In Revera, their inner fire could be understood as more than destruction: a spark of transformation, creation, and change.

## **Gnome**  
Small, inventive folk characterized by curiosity, cleverness, and an affinity for creating and understanding things. They fit beautifully into Revera's tradition of artisans, scholars, tinkers, and eccentric inventors. Where others see a complicated problem, a gnome tends to see an interesting afternoon.

## **Aetheris**  
Celestial-descended people marked by radiant features and an unmistakably otherworldly presence. Their ancestry carries a natural association with divine light and celestial power. In Revera, this gives us a fascinating people to place within the broader question of what happens when mortal blood carries something that feels unmistakably divine.
