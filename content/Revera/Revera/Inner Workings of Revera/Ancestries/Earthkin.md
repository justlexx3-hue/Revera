**Shaper:** [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth, the Shaper]] 
**Gifter:** [[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel, the Lifebringer]]

Earthkin are humanoids whose bodies combine flesh and earth. Stone, clay, sand, soil, and crystal can all become part of their physical form, ranging from subtle mineral features to bodies dominated by earthen material.

Vareth shaped them from the **enduring substance of Revera**, while Amariel gifted them a connection between **earth and life**. Their presence is particularly strong in the mountains, deep forests, and ancient places where the foundations of the world remain exposed.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Stoneskin:** Gain a permanent **+1 Armor Score** and **+1 to your damage thresholds** at character creation.
- **Immovable:** While touching the ground, you cannot be **lifted or moved against your will**.
