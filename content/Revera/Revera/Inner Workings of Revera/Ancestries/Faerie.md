**Shaper:** [[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor, the Inspirer]]
**Gifter:** [[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana, the Threadkeeper]]

Faeries are small, winged people with insect-like features. Their appearance can range from subtly unusual to distinctly insectoid, with possibilities including compound eyes, chitin, additional limbs, stingers, and other traits. They undergo a metamorphosis during childhood that establishes much of their adult form.

Lysandor shaped the Faeries through **dreams and imagination**, while Lyriana gifted them an affinity for **possibility and changing paths**. Their presence is particularly common in Revera's hidden forests, moonlit places, and regions where the ordinary world feels slightly less certain.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Luckbender:** Once per session, after an action roll by you or a willing ally within Close range, spend **3 Hope** to reroll the Duality Dice.
- **Wings:** You can fly. While flying, mark **1 Stress** after an attack against you to gain **+2 Evasion** against that attack.
