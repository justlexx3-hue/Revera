**Shaper:** [[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos, the Masked]]
**Gifter:** [[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira, the Wayfarer]]

Katari are feline humanoids with retractable claws, slit pupils, triangular ears, whiskers, and heightened senses. Their fur and markings vary greatly, producing a wide range of appearances.

Kaelos shaped the Katari with **instinct and adaptability**, while Elira gifted them **independence and the desire to explore**. They are naturally suited to navigating both the wild places and crowded settlements of Revera.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Feline Instincts:** When making an Agility Roll, spend **2 Hope** to reroll your Hope Die.
- **Retracting Claws:** Make an Agility attack against a target within Melee range. On a success, the target becomes **temporarily Vulnerable**.
