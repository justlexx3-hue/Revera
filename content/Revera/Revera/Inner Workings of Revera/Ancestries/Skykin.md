**Shaper:** [[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira, the Wayfarer]]
**Gifter:** [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion, the Prime Light]]

Skykin are humanoids whose bodies combine flesh and air. Their appearance can carry the colors of dawn, sunset, or night, while wispy hair, translucent features, and constantly moving air reflect their elemental nature.

Elira shaped the Skykin from **wind, movement, and freedom**, while Elarion gifted them the **lightness and hope of the open sky**. They are naturally drawn toward high places, open horizons, and the endless winds of Revera.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Gale Force:** Mark **1 Stress** to create a gust that carries you or a Very Close ally up to **Very Far range**. You can also control your falling speed.
- **Eye of the Storm:** Spend **2 Hope** to give yourself or an ally within Melee range **+1 Evasion** until you take Severe damage or use this feature again.
