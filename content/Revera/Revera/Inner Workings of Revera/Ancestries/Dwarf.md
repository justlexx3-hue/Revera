**Shaper:** [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth, the Shaper]] 
**Gifter:** [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon, the Arbiter]]

Dwarves are short, broad, powerfully built people with dense musculature, thick hair, and naturally resilient skin and nails. Their bodies can accommodate gemstones, tattoos, piercings, and other forms of adornment, often making craftsmanship an integral part of their appearance.

Vareth shaped the Dwarves with **craftsmanship and endurance**, while Rheon gifted them **discipline and responsibility**. The mountains surrounding Cael'Vareth remain home to some of the oldest dwarven traditions in Revera.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Thick Skin:** For Minor damage, you can mark **2 Stress instead of 1 Hit Point**.
- **Increased Fortitude:** Spend **3 Hope** to halve incoming physical damage.
