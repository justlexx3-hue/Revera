**Shaper:** [[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana, the Threadkeeper]]
**Gifter:** [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion, the Prime Light]]

Firbolgs are tall, muscular bovine people with broad noses, drooping ears, fur, and often horns. Their features may resemble cattle, bison, oxen, or other great bovine creatures, giving the ancestry considerable physical variety.

Lyriana shaped the Firbolgs as beings connected to the **greater tapestry of life**, giving them an awareness of their place within the world. Elarion gifted them **light, hope, and resilience**, creating a people well suited to enduring difficult environments. Many Firbolgs are drawn to ancient forests, open wilderness, and places where the natural world remains strong.

**Ancestry Features**

- **Charge:** After succeeding on an Agility Roll to move from Far or Very Far into Melee range, mark **1 Stress** to deal **1d12 physical damage** to all targets within Melee range.
- **Unshakable:** When you would mark Stress, roll a d6. On a **6**, you avoid marking it.
