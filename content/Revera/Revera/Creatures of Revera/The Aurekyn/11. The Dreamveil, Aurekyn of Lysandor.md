# Dreamveil

_Aurekyn of [[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer| Lysandor, the Inspirer]]_

## Classification

**Species:** Aurekyn (Exalted Beast)

**Titles:** The Moonborne, Weaver of Reveries

---
## Overview

The **Dreamveil** is the divine embodiment of dreams, imagination, inspiration, and wonder. Created by Lysandor, it wanders beneath moonlit skies where the boundary between reality and possibility grows thin. Artists, poets, inventors, and dreamers speak of fleeting encounters with the Dreamveil, believing its presence plants the first spark from which every masterpiece is born.

---
## Appearance

The Dreamveil is an ethereal feline Aurekyn with a graceful, weightless form, soft ivory fur, and immense translucent moth-like wings that fold around its body like a moonlit cloak. Long ribbon-like antennae sweep elegantly behind its head, while luminous opalescent eyes gaze upward as though forever following dreams beyond mortal sight.

As it moves, shimmering dream motes drift from its wings, briefly taking the shapes of birds, flowers, melodies, castles, and impossible wonders before dissolving back into light.

---
## Divine Gifts

- Inspires creativity and imagination.
- Guides peaceful dreams and meaningful visions.
- Rekindles hope in those who have lost it.
- Reveals possibilities hidden beyond ordinary thought.
- Brings moments of quiet clarity before great acts of creation.

---
## Temperament

Gentle, curious, and endlessly serene.

The Dreamveil is drawn to moments of quiet contemplation rather than places of grandeur. It often appears to those standing at the edge of inspiration, lingering only long enough to awaken a forgotten dream before vanishing with the morning light.

---
## Mythology

When the world was young, Lysandor gathered every unspoken story, unfinished melody, and impossible dream that mortals had yet to imagine. From these fragments of pure possibility, he wove moonlight into living wings and gave them the shape of the first Dreamveil.

It has wandered the world ever since, carrying the seeds of every future masterpiece upon its wings.

---
## Symbolism

The Dreamveil represents:

- Dreams
- Imagination
- Inspiration
- Wonder
- Hope
- Possibility

---
## Common Beliefs

- A glimpse of the Dreamveil is said to precede great works of art or discovery.
- Sleeping beneath its passing wings brings vivid dreams remembered for a lifetime.
- The dream motes it leaves behind are believed to become the first spark of every great idea.
- No two people ever describe its wings in exactly the same way.

> _"Every dream begins as a whisper upon its wings."_

---
# Legacy

### **Dreamsilk**

On exceedingly rare occasions, a single whisker sheds instead of fading.

Known as **Dreamsilk**, these sacred relics shimmer softly beneath moonlight. Those who keep one close often find their dreams unusually vivid, their imagination renewed, and their hearts filled with quiet inspiration during times of doubt.
