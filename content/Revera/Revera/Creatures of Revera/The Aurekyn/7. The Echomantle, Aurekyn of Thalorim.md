# Echomantle

_Aurekyn of [[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim, the Archivist]]_

## Classification

**Species:** Aurekyn (Exalted Beast)

**Titles:** Keeper of Forgotten Truths, Warden of the Eternal Archive

---------
## Overview

The **Echomantle** is the divine embodiment of memory, wisdom, and preserved knowledge. Created by Thalorim to ensure that no truth worthy of remembrance is ever truly lost, it wanders the hidden libraries and forgotten places of Revera, silently safeguarding the world’s history.

Owls are believed to be mortal reflections of the Echomantle’s quiet vigilance.

--------
## Appearance

The Echomantle is a majestic owl-like Aurekyn with an elegant, upright posture and luminous ivory plumage accented by deep indigo. Rather than ordinary feathers, each plume resembles translucent vellum inscribed with glowing calligraphy that subtly shifts as new knowledge enters the world.

Its immense wings, known as **The Living Archive**, unfold into countless manuscript-like feathers covered in forgotten languages, celestial maps, and ancient histories. Feathers that drift free become radiant pages before dissolving into golden light.

A crown of towering crystalline sapphire **Quills** rises from behind its head, each etched with ancient runes. Tiny golden letters drift from their tips before fading into the air.

Its piercing sapphire eyes reflect shifting constellations, carrying the quiet wisdom of one who remembers every story ever told.

---------
## Divine Gifts

- Preserves forgotten knowledge and lost histories.
- Restores memories thought to be gone forever.
- Reveals hidden truths to those who seek wisdom with humility.
- Guards sacred libraries and places of learning from corruption.

----------
## Temperament

Patient, contemplative, and impossibly wise. The Echomantle neither offers knowledge freely nor withholds it out of pride. It believes every truth has its proper time to be discovered, and that wisdom cannot exist without understanding.

--------
## Mythology

When the first stories began to fade from mortal memory, Thalorim gathered the final echoes of every forgotten word and breathed them into the first Echomantle.

Its Pages have continued to record the history of Revera ever since.

Some believe every significant event is written upon its wings before it even comes to pass.

-----
## Symbolism

The Echomantle represents:

- Knowledge
- Wisdom
- Memory
- Truth
- Discovery

-----
## Common Beliefs

- A Page naturally shed by an Echomantle contains a single memory from Revera’s history. Those who touch it experience that moment exactly as it occurred.
- Libraries blessed by an Echomantle are said to never truly lose their knowledge, no matter how many books are burned.
- Scholars believe that every unanswered question already exists somewhere among the Echomantle’s endless Pages.

>_“Ink may fade. Stone may crumble. But the Echomantle remembers.”_

# Legacy

## **Living Pages**

A naturally shed Page allows the bearer to relive one forgotten moment from Revera's history. Each Page contains a unique memory and loses its script once its story has been witnessed.
