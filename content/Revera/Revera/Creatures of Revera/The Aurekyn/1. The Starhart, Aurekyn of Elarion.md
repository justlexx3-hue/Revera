# Starhart

_Aurekyn of [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion, the Prime Light]]_

## Classification

**Species:** Aurekyn (Exalted Beast)

**Titles:** Beacon of the First Dawn, Warden of the Ancient Grove

---
## Overview

The **Starhart** is the divine embodiment of hope, guidance, and the First Light. It is believed to have been the first Aurekyn created by Elarion at the dawn of Creation. Stags are said to be mortal reflections of the Starhart, not its ancestors.

---
## Appearance

A majestic, elk-like creature standing nearly 3 meters (10 ft) at the shoulder. Its ivory-white coat shimmers with faint constellations at night, while its living antlers are formed from ancient wood intertwined with luminous crystal. Its golden eyes radiate warmth and wisdom.

---
## Divine Gifts

- Purifies corrupted land and water.
- Encourages life and growth wherever it roams.
- Dispels darkness with the radiant **First Light** carried within its antlers.
- Inspires courage and hope in nearby creatures.

---
## Temperament

Gentle, noble, and deeply intelligent. A Starhart avoids violence whenever possible, but stands unwavering against corruption and darkness. It cannot be tamed or commanded, choosing only those whose hearts embody Elarion's virtues.

---
## Mythology

According to temple doctrine, Elarion shaped the first Starhart from the roots of the first tree, the light of Astera, and a fallen star. When a Starhart reaches the end of its existence, its body dissolves into starlight, leaving behind its antlers, which take root and grow into sacred **Dawnwood Trees**.

---
## Symbolism

The Starhart represents:

- Hope
- Wisdom
- Noble leadership
- Renewal
- The triumph of light over darkness

---
## Common Beliefs

- Seeing a Starhart is considered a blessing from Elarion.
- Its appearance often foretells great change or the rise of a worthy hero.
- No one knows how many Starharts exist. Ancient scripture simply states:

> _"The Starharts are as many as the First Light requires."_

# Legacy

## Dawnwood Groves

Where a Starhart's antlers are shed or its hooves linger, sacred groves of Dawnwood trees take root. Their wood glows faintly at dawn and is prized for crafting holy relics of Elarion.
