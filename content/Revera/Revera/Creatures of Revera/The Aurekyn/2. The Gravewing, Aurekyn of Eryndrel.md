# Gravewing

_Aurekyn of [[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel, the Sheperd]]_

## Classification

**Species:** Aurekyn (Exalted Beast)

**Titles:** Shepherd of the Last Path, Keeper of Silent Wings

---
## Overview

The **Gravewing** is the divine guide of departed souls, created by Eryndrel to lead the dead safely beyond the Veil. Though feared by many, the Gravewing is not an omen of death, but a promise that no soul must walk its final journey alone.

Ravens are believed to be mortal echoes of the Gravewing, carrying fragments of its watchful nature.

---
## Appearance

A towering avian creature with a wingspan exceeding 8 meters (26 ft). Its plumage appears black from afar, yet up close each feather reveals a sky filled with distant stars. Its face is smooth and mask-like, with softly glowing silver eyes that reflect memories rather than light. A mantle of pale feathers encircles its neck like a ceremonial cloak, while long tail plumes drift behind it like smoke.

---
## Divine Gifts

- Guides wandering souls safely beyond the Veil.
- Brings peace to the dying and comfort to those in mourning.
- Reveals hidden truths through memories.
- Protects sacred burial grounds from corruption and undeath.

---
## Temperament

Silent, patient, and compassionate. A Gravewing neither judges nor condemns. It is said to remain beside those whose time has come, offering calm in their final moments. It rarely speaks, yet its presence alone eases fear.

---
## Mythology

When the first mortal died, Eryndrel saw that no path yet existed beyond life. From the first night sky and the quiet between heartbeats, he shaped the Gravewing to carry souls safely into the beyond. Since then, every peaceful passing is said to occur beneath its unseen wings.

---
## Symbolism

The Gravewing represents:

- Peaceful passing
- Remembrance
- Compassion
- Acceptance
- The eternal journey

---
## Common Beliefs

- Seeing a Gravewing before death is considered a blessing, not a curse.
- A single black feather found after a funeral is believed to be a sign that the departed has safely crossed the Veil.
- Gravewings are never seen hunting or feeding, leading many to believe they exist entirely between the mortal world and the next.

> _"Fear not the shadow of the Gravewing, for where it watches, no soul is ever lost."_
# Legacy

## Veilfeathers

A Gravewing occasionally sheds a celestial feather. When placed beside the dying, it eases fear and helps guide the soul peacefully into Eryndrel's embrace.
