# Deepwake

_Aurekyn of [[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis the Tidebearer]]_

## Classification

**Species:** Aurekyn (Exalted Beast)

**Titles:** Warden of the Endless Sea, Keeper of the Deep Currents

---
## Overview

The **Deepwake** is the divine embodiment of the oceans, tides, and the eternal flow of the sea. Created by Syrethis when the first waters embraced the shores of Revera, it wanders the deepest oceans, ensuring the seas remain in harmony.

Whales are believed to be mortal reflections of the Deepwake's immense grace.

---
## Appearance

A colossal marine Aurekyn of impossible scale, the Deepwake possesses a long, elegant body that blends the majesty of a whale, the fluidity of a sea serpent, and the soaring fins of a manta ray. Its sapphire-blue hide shimmers with silver beneath the waves, while coral reefs, glowing anemones, and pearl-like shells naturally flourish along its back. Its luminous turquoise eyes shine like lanterns in the abyss, and every graceful movement leaves glowing currents drifting through the sea.

---
## Divine Gifts

- Guides the tides and ocean currents.
- Calms storms and restores balance to troubled seas.
- Protects marine life and hidden underwater sanctuaries.
- Guides respectful sailors safely across the ocean.

---
## Temperament

Serene, patient, and older than memory itself. The Deepwake rarely surfaces, choosing instead to watch the world from beneath the waves. Though gentle by nature, it becomes a force of overwhelming power when the seas or their creatures are threatened.

---
## Mythology

When Syrethis filled the world with oceans, the waters lacked purpose. From the first wave, the deepest abyss, and the breath of the open sea, Syrethis created the Deepwake to guide every current and every tide until the end of time.

---
## Symbolism

The Deepwake represents:

- Balance
- Adaptability
- Exploration
- Protection
- The endless rhythm of the sea

---
## Common Beliefs

- Calm seas after a great storm are said to be the wake of a passing Deepwake.
- Sailors believe glimpsing its glowing form guarantees a safe voyage.
- Some islanders claim entire coral atolls have grown upon the backs of ancient sleeping Deepwakes.

> _"The sea remembers every Deepwake that has ever passed."_

# Legacy

## Tidepearls

Rare pearls formed where a Deepwake sings beneath the sea. Sailors believe they calm storms and help navigators always find their way home.
