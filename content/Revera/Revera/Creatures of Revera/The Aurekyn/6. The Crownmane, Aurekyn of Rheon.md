# Crownmane

_Aurekyn of [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon, the Arbiter]]_

## Classification

**Species:** Aurekyn (Exalted Beast)

**Titles:** King of the Golden Throne, Warden of the Just Crown

---
## Overview

The **Crownmane** is the divine embodiment of justice, honor, and rightful rule. Created by Rheon to remind rulers that true authority is earned through wisdom and integrity, the Crownmane appears only in times when the balance of justice is at stake.

Lions are believed to be mortal reflections of the Crownmane's noble bearing.

---
## Appearance

The Crownmane possesses a powerful four-legged form with broad shoulders and graceful, deliberate movements. Its body is covered in short, radiant fur that gleams like polished bronze beneath the sun.

Its magnificent mane is unlike any mortal creature's. Thousands of flowing strands of golden light weave together like the rays of a sunrise, forming a living crown around its head and shoulders. Within the mane, tiny fragments of crystal glimmer like stars, and when the Crownmane speaks or roars, the entire mantle shines with divine brilliance.

Its amber eyes are impossibly calm, seeing not status or wealth, but the truth within every heart.

---
## Divine Gifts

- Reveals truth and exposes deception.
- Inspires courage, honor, and selflessness.
- Calms conflict between the just.
- Drives back tyranny and corruption through its mere presence.

---
## Temperament

Confident, noble, and unwavering. The Crownmane never seeks dominion for itself, believing that power exists only to serve others. It does not judge by titles or bloodlines, but by character alone.

---
## Mythology

When the first kingdoms arose, Rheon saw that crowns alone could not make worthy rulers. From the first sunrise, the purest gold, and the courage of those who defended others, he forged the first Crownmane. It was sent into the world not to rule, but to remind rulers what they should become.

---
## Symbolism

The Crownmane represents:

- Justice
- Honor
- Courage
- Leadership
- Selfless authority

---
## Common Beliefs

- A ruler who earns the blessing of a Crownmane is destined to lead wisely.
- It is said that no liar can meet the Crownmane's gaze for long.
- Some ancient thrones are believed to have been carved where a Crownmane once chose to rest.

> _"A crown commands obedience. A Crownmane inspires loyalty."_

# Legacy

## **Crownfire**

Where a Crownmane stands in judgment, golden grasses grow that never bend to the wind. Kings often place a single blade beneath a new throne as a reminder that authority must remain steadfast.
