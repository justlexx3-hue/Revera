# Bloomheart

_Aurekyn of [[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel, the Lifebringer]]_

## Classification

**Species:** Aurekyn (Exalted Beast)

**Titles:** Heart of the Wilds, Mother of the First Bloom

---
## Overview

The **Bloomheart** is the divine embodiment of life, growth, and renewal, created by Amariel when the first seeds took root in the newborn world. Wherever it wanders, forests awaken, flowers bloom, and life flourishes.

Deer are believed to be mortal reflections of the Bloomheart's gentle grace.

---
## Appearance

A majestic four-legged creature with a graceful, elegant form. Its body is woven from smooth living wood covered in moss, flowering vines, and emerald leaves that shift with the seasons. A crown of flowering branches rises from its head, alive with blossoms, fruit, and nesting birds. Butterflies, bees, and woodland creatures are naturally drawn to its presence, while its emerald eyes shine with the warmth of spring itself.

---

## Divine Gifts

- Restores barren land to fertile wilderness.
- Encourages the growth of forests and crops.
- Heals wounded plants and animals.
- Inspires harmony between all living things.

---
## Temperament

Gentle, nurturing, and endlessly patient. The Bloomheart rarely confronts destruction with force, instead allowing life to reclaim what has been lost. It is said that no creature feels fear in its presence.

---
## Mythology

When Amariel breathed life into the first forests, she shaped the Bloomheart from the oldest root, the first blossom, and the morning dew, charging it to wander the wild places so that life would always endure beyond hardship.

---
## Symbolism

The Bloomheart represents:

- Life
- Growth
- Renewal
- Fertility
- Harmony with nature

---
## Common Beliefs

- A forest blessed by a Bloomheart will thrive for generations.
- Flowers blooming out of season are said to mark its passing.
- Woodland shrines leave offerings of seeds, fresh water, and wildflowers in hopes of receiving Amariel's blessing.

> _"Where the Bloomheart walks, spring is never far behind."_

# Legacy

## Heartblooms

Where a Bloomheart rests, flowers bloom that never wilt. Their seeds are treasured by healers and gardeners, and entire sanctuaries are said to have sprung from a single Heartbloom.
