# Concordance

_Aurekyn of [[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra, the Bondweave]]_

## Classification

**Species:** Aurekyn (Exalted Beast)

**Titles:** The Bondwalker, Weaver of Hearts

---
## Overview

The **Concordance** is the divine embodiment of companionship, trust, and the sacred bonds that unite souls. Fashioned by Calendra at the dawn of the world, it wanders ancient roads and forgotten woodland paths, quietly strengthening the invisible ties between families, friends, lovers, and kindred spirits.

Unlike many divine beings, the Concordance does not seek reverence. It seeks connection. It is said that those who encounter one are never truly alone for as long as it chooses to walk beside them.

Graceful sighthounds are believed to be distant mortal reflections of the Concordance's loyalty and gentle spirit.

---
## Appearance

The Concordance is an elegant four-legged Aurekyn with the refined proportions of a celestial sighthound. Its body is impossibly graceful, with long limbs, a slender neck, and a calm, noble posture that seems to invite companionship rather than command attention.

Its form is not made of flesh, but of thousands of living strands woven together into a seamless tapestry. Ivory and crimson fibers intertwine throughout its body, forming every muscle, tendon, and contour as though Calendra herself crafted it upon a divine loom.

Loose strands gently unravel from its woven form before effortlessly weaving themselves back into its body, never fraying or breaking.

Behind its head floats the **Halo Weave**, a radiant circle of interwoven golden filaments resembling a celestial loom forever spinning unseen threads.

Its warm amber eyes carry quiet understanding, meeting every gaze with kindness rather than judgment.

---
## Divine Gifts

- Strengthens bonds of trust, friendship, family, and fellowship.
- Blesses vows and promises freely given.
- Encourages reconciliation between estranged hearts.
- Reveals the unseen connections that bind all living things.

---
## Temperament

Gentle, compassionate, and endlessly patient.

The Concordance is drawn to those journeying alone, often appearing without warning to silently accompany travelers along lonely roads. It asks for nothing, speaks no words, and departs just as quietly once its purpose has been fulfilled.

It cannot create love or loyalty where none exists. Instead, it nurtures the bonds already present, reminding mortals of the strength found in one another.

---
## Mythology

When the Twelve first shaped Revera, Calendra saw that even the most beautiful world would remain empty if its people stood apart.

Upon her divine loom she wove two eternal threads: one of **Trust**, the other of **Devotion**. As they crossed, they gave birth to the first Concordance, whose woven body became the living symbol of every sincere bond formed thereafter.

Wherever a Concordance walks, invisible threads between living souls briefly shimmer into view before fading once more.

---
## Symbolism

The Concordance represents:

- Companionship
- Trust
- Unity
- Devotion
- Fellowship

---
## Common Beliefs

- To meet a Concordance while traveling is believed to foretell the beginning of a lifelong friendship.
- Those who walk beside a Concordance are said to never lose their way, no matter how uncertain the road ahead.
- Many wedding processions weave crimson and ivory ribbons together in remembrance of Calendra's first weaving.
- It is said a Concordance only appears when two destinies are meant to cross.

> _"No road is truly lonely while the Bondwalker walks beside you."_

# Legacy

### **Heartweave Threads**

On exceedingly rare occasions, a single luminous strand naturally loosens from a Concordance's woven body. These sacred **Heartweave Threads** never fray, fade, or weaken.

They are woven into wedding cords, peace treaties, family heirlooms, and sacred vows. While every promise bound to a Heartweave Thread is honored sincerely, it glows with a warm golden radiance.

Should those vows be willingly betrayed, its light quietly fades forever, becoming a solemn reminder that trust cannot be mended by magic alone.
