# Skystrider

_Aurekyn of [[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira, the Wayfarer]]_

## Classification

**Species:** Aurekyn (Exalted Beast)

**Titles:** The Horizon's Herald, Windborne Wanderer

---
## Overview

The **Skystrider** is the divine embodiment of freedom, exploration, adventure, and the endless horizon. Created by Elira, it soars where the sky meets the world, forever chasing distant lands and unseen possibilities. Sailors, explorers, and wanderers believe that every great journey begins beneath the shadow of its wings.

---
## Appearance

The Skystrider is a graceful avian Aurekyn inspired by the elegant form of a swallow, with a streamlined body, impossibly long wings, and a deeply forked tail that trails into flowing ribbons of living wind. Its ivory plumage shimmers with soft sky-blue and silver hues, while the edges of its wings dissolve into visible currents of air that seem to merge seamlessly with the heavens.

Its brilliant sapphire eyes are filled with boundless curiosity, forever searching beyond the next horizon.

---
## Divine Gifts

- Guides travelers safely across land, sea, and sky.
- Inspires courage to embrace the unknown.
- Carries favorable winds to those who journey with honest purpose.
- Encourages freedom, discovery, and new beginnings.
- Reveals forgotten paths and hidden places.

---
## Temperament

Restless, joyful, and endlessly curious.

The Skystrider rarely lingers in one place for long. It delights in discovering unseen lands and often appears beside those standing upon the threshold of a new adventure, urging them to take the first step.

---
## Mythology

When the world was complete, Elira looked beyond its borders and wondered what lay over every horizon yet unseen. She gathered the first morning breeze, the highest cirrus cloud, and the courage of every soul willing to venture into the unknown. From these she shaped the first Skystrider and released it into the endless heavens.

Since that day, no wind has ever truly been without purpose.

---
## Symbolism

The Skystrider represents:

- Freedom
- Exploration
- Adventure
- Discovery
- Hope
- Endless Possibility

---
## Common Beliefs

- Seeing a Skystrider before setting out is considered the greatest blessing an explorer can receive.
- Those who follow its flight often discover places no map has ever recorded.
- It is said the Skystrider never flies in circles, only toward new horizons.
- A single feather carried by the wind is believed to herald the beginning of a life-changing journey.

> _"The horizon is not an end, but an invitation."_

---
# Legacy

## Windplumes

On exceedingly rare occasions, one of the Skystrider's flowing feathers condenses into solid form.

Known as **Windplumes**, these sacred relics are impossibly light and forever warm with a gentle breeze. They are treasured by navigators and adventurers, for it is said they always stir toward the path one is truly meant to follow.
