# Worldhide

_Aurekyn of [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth, the Shaper]]_

## Classification

**Species:** Aurekyn (Exalted Beast)

**Titles:** The Living Mesa, Bearer of the First Mountains

---
## Overview

The **Worldhide** is the divine embodiment of creation, endurance, and the shaping of the earth. Fashioned by Vareth at the dawn of the world, it wanders the great deserts and canyons where mountains first took form, its silent passage sculpting the land over the course of ages.

Giant tortoises are believed to be distant mortal reflections of the Worldhide's patient spirit and timeless endurance.

---
## Appearance

The Worldhide is a colossal four-legged Aurekyn whose form resembles a living mountain more than any mortal creature.

Its immense body is sculpted from layered red sandstone, weathered cliffs, and ancient canyon walls, shaped by countless ages of wind and time. Rising from its broad back is a vast natural mesa crowned with buttes, hoodoos, balanced rocks, and weathered stone arches, so seamlessly formed that it is often mistaken for part of the surrounding landscape.

Its long, graceful neck supports a broad sandstone head with kind amber eyes that glow softly like sunlight passing through translucent quartz. Its expression is calm and contemplative, carrying the quiet wisdom of geological ages.

The Worldhide bears no visible mouth or teeth. It neither hunts nor feeds as mortal creatures do, instead drawing strength from the earth itself.

---
## Divine Gifts

- Shapes mountains, valleys, and canyons simply through its presence.
- Strengthens stone and foundations blessed by Vareth.
- Carries the memory of the world's first landscapes within its living body.
- Brings stability wherever it chooses to rest.

---
## Temperament

Patient, gentle, and immeasurably old. The Worldhide has no concept of haste, for it measures time in the rise of mountains and the carving of rivers. It rarely moves, often remaining still for centuries, allowing the land to grow around it until even kingdoms mistake it for part of the world itself.

It embodies Vareth's greatest lesson:

> _The strongest creations are never rushed._

---
## Mythology

When the world was still soft beneath the hands of the gods, Vareth shaped the first mountains from living stone. From those sacred peaks she awakened the first Worldhide and entrusted it with carrying the bones of Creation across the land.

Wherever a Worldhide chose to rest, the earth slowly rose to meet it. Hills became cliffs, cliffs became mountains, and rivers carved new paths around its sleeping form.

Many of Revera's oldest mesas are believed to be places where a Worldhide once dreamed.

---
## Symbolism

The Worldhide represents:

- Creation
- Endurance
- Stability
- Patience
- Craftsmanship

---
## Common Beliefs

- Settlements built upon stone blessed by a Worldhide are said to stand for generations without faltering.
- Builders and masons pray to Vareth before laying a foundation, asking for the steadfastness of the Worldhide.
- Some ancient mesas are believed to be sleeping Worldhides, and wise travelers avoid disturbing them.

> _"The mountains are not eternal. They are simply where a Worldhide chose to rest."_

---
# Legacy

## **Foundation Stones**

On exceedingly rare occasions, a fragment of weathered stone naturally separates from a Worldhide. These **Foundation Stones** are considered among the holiest relics of Vareth.

Neither chisel nor storm can crack them, and structures built upon them are said to endure for millennia. Many of Revera's oldest temples, bridges, and halls claim a Foundation Stone lies hidden within their foundations, a silent gift from the Shaper's Aurekyn.
