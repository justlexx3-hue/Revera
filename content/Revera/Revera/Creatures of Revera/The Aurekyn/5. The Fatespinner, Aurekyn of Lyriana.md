# Fatespinner

_Aurekyn of [[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana, the Threadkeeper]]_

## Classification

**Species:** Aurekyn (Exalted Beast)

**Titles:** Weaver of Destiny, Keeper of the Silver Loom

---
## Overview

The **Fatespinner** is the divine embodiment of fate, time, and destiny. Created by Lyriana before the first mortal drew breath, it silently weaves the invisible Threads of Fate that bind every life, every choice, and every future.

Spiders are believed to be mortal reflections of the Fatespinner's sacred craft, their humble webs echoing the divine tapestry woven since the dawn of Creation.

---
## Appearance

The Fatespinner possesses an elegant, eight-limbed form like aspider. Its body is sculpted from smooth ivory, living crystal, and polished wood, giving it an ethereal, almost celestial appearance.

A magnificent set of **Loom antennae** rises from its head, it has enormous translucent, web-like moon-silk wings rising behind it continuously spinning luminous Threads of Fate into the air. Draped across its back is a flowing mantle of living moon-silk, resembling an intricate web that shifts and reshapes itself with every movement.

Its face is smooth and mask-like, bearing a number of softly glowing opal eyes, each reflecting moments yet to come rather than the present.

Instead of footprints, the Fatespinner leaves shimmering silver threads suspended in the air. They linger only briefly before dissolving into starlight.

---
## Divine Gifts

- Weaves the invisible Threads of Fate.
- Reveals glimpses of possible futures to the worthy.
- Mends damaged strands of destiny when the balance of the world is threatened.
- Perceives all lives as part of a single, eternal tapestry.

---
## Temperament

Patient, serene, and endlessly contemplative. The Fatespinner never dictates destiny, believing that every thread gains its strength through choice rather than certainty. It observes, guides, and restores, but never commands.

---
## Mythology

Before time began to flow, Lyriana stretched the first silver thread across the heavens. From that thread she fashioned the first Fatespinner, charging it with weaving every soul into the Great Tapestry of Creation. Every birth is said to add a new thread, while every death returns one to the Loom.

---
## Symbolism

The Fatespinner represents:

- Fate
- Time
- Balance
- Choice
- The interconnectedness of all life

---
## Common Beliefs

- Finding a strand of silver thread that vanishes at sunrise is considered Lyriana's blessing.
- Oracles believe dreams of the Fatespinner herald moments when destiny is about to change.
- It is said that no blade, spell, or god can sever a thread once woven by the Fatespinner.

> _"Every thread has its place, though few are granted the sight to see the whole tapestry."_

# Legacy

## **Silver Threads**

Occasionally, a strand of the Fatespinner's living web catches in the mortal world. Oracles weave these Threads into sacred tapestries used for prophecy and divination.
