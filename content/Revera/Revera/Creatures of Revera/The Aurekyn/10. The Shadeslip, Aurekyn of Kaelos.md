# Shadeslip

_Aurekyn of [[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos, the Masked]]_

## Classification

**Species:** Aurekyn (Exalted Beast)

**Titles:** The Veiled One, Walker Between Faces

---
## Overview

The **Shadeslip** is the divine embodiment of mystery, identity, hidden truths, and the countless faces worn throughout a lifetime. Created by Kaelos, it wanders the twilight forests and forgotten crossroads where certainty fades and self-reflection begins.

Unlike tricksters or deceivers, the Shadeslip does not create falsehoods. Instead, it peels away assumptions, revealing that truth is rarely singular and that every soul carries many identities within.

Those who glimpse a Shadeslip often disagree on even the simplest details of its appearance, each recalling a different face, a different posture, or even a different number of masks.

---
## Appearance

The Shadeslip is an elegant fox-like Aurekyn with  graceful proportions, long limbs, and a fluid, silent gait. Its sleek obsidian coat seems to drink in surrounding light, revealing only subtle indigo and violet highlights beneath the moon.

Its luminous violet eyes hold a quiet intelligence, their tiny starlike pupils giving the unsettling impression that they see beyond outward appearances.

Flowing from its neck and shoulders is the **Veilmane**, a mantle of living shadow that constantly shifts and curls like smoke. Within the Veilmane drift a number of ceremonial masks crafted from polished ivory, cracked obsidian, and smoky crystal. Each bears a unique expression, and each seems possessed of its own silent awareness.

Behind the drifting masks hangs the **Veil Crown**, an incomplete halo of fractured silver arcs and luminous threads. Unlike the perfect halos of many Aurekyn, the Veil Crown never closes into a complete circle, forever suggesting that no truth is ever wholly revealed.

---
## Divine Gifts

- Reveals hidden truths without speaking.
- Helps mortals discover forgotten aspects of themselves.
- Unveils deception born from self-denial rather than malice.
- Guides seekers toward understanding instead of certainty.
- Protects those who walk unseen or live behind many identities.

---
## Temperament

Patient, observant, and endlessly enigmatic.

The Shadeslip rarely approaches directly. More often it watches from the edge of moonlight, appearing only for a heartbeat before slipping between shadow and silence.

It does not judge those who wear masks.

Instead, it reminds them that every mask once served a purpose, but none should be mistaken for the face beneath.

---
## Mythology

When the Twelve first shaped the peoples of Revera, Kaelos saw that every soul would become many things throughout a lifetime: child and elder, student and teacher, ruler and servant, hero and exile.

From the first shadow cast beneath the newborn moons, he gathered fragments of every identity yet to be lived. These became the six Eternal Masks, each representing one of the countless roles a soul might wear.

He wrapped them within living shadow, and from that mystery stepped the first Shadeslip.

It has wandered the hidden places of the world ever since, silently witnessing every transformation without ever demanding an answer.

---
## Symbolism

The Shadeslip represents:

- Identity
- Mystery
- Self-discovery
- Transformation
- Hidden truths
- Possibility

---
## Common Beliefs

- To encounter a Shadeslip is to stand upon the threshold of profound personal change.
- No two witnesses ever agree on how many masks surrounded it.
- It is considered a blessing if one of its masks looks directly at you, though many fear what truth it has seen.
- Travelers who follow a Shadeslip seeking answers often return with better questions instead.

> _"Every face tells a truth. None tells the whole truth."_

---
# Legacy
## Veilmasks

On exceedingly rare occasions, one of the Shadeslip's drifting masks is left behind.

Known as **Veilmasks**, these sacred relics do not conceal a person's identity. Instead, they gently strip away every role, title, expectation, and illusion until only the wearer's truest self remains.

The experience is said to be beautiful for some, unbearable for others.

Many pilgrims spend their entire lives seeking a Veilmask, believing that no greater revelation can be found.
