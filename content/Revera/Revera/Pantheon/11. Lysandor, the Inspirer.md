[[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor]] is the god of **dreams, inspiration, and imagination**. Artists, musicians, storytellers, inventors, and prophets all claim to hear his whispers while they dream. To Lysandor, every great creation begins as a possibility imagined in the mind, and every masterpiece begins as **one quiet spark of inspiration**.

---

# His Place Among the Twelve

The gods of Revera aren't divided into conventional good and evil. They embody fundamental aspects of existence, and they don't wage divine wars against one another.

[[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor]] belongs to **The Endless Horizon**, alongside:

- [[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis, the Tidebearer]]
- [[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira, the Wayfarer]]

This triad represents the forces that call mortals beyond what they already know.

A simple way of understanding the three is:

- **Syrethis calls mortals toward the unknown.**
- **Lysandor inspires them to imagine what might be found there.**
- **Elira gives them the courage to go and discover it.**

Together, they represent **wonder, possibility, and discovery**.

# Lysandor and Inspiration
![[Symbol Lysandor.jpeg|right|600]]
[[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor]] governs dreams, inspiration, imagination and posibility. He is the quiet voice behind the artist's first idea, the sudden image that appears in a dream, and the impossible thought that eventually becomes something real. He gives mortals the spark that allows them to create it.

A painter still has to lift the brush. A musician still has to play the first note. An inventor still has to build the machine. [[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor]] simply places the possibility in their mind.

---

# Lysandor's First Descent

[[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor's]] first descent into the mortal world occurred at the [[Monestary of the Seven Winds]]. There, his first song was heard by the mortals who witnessed his arrival. The song has never truly disappeared. Even centuries later, it can still be heard faintly within the winds surrounding the monastery, becoming the **Sacred Remnant of his Descent**.

No two listeners describe the melody in quite the same way. Some hear a lullaby. Others hear orchestral music. Some swear they hear voices telling stories they have never heard before. The monks teach that this is not because the song changes. It is because [[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor's]] inspiration sounds different to every soul that receives it.

---

# The Bloodline of the Seven Winds

During his descent, [[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor]] blessed a mortal who became the first ancestor of the ruling bloodline associated with the Monastery of the Seven Winds. That lineage continues into the present day, meaning its rulers carry **[[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor's]] divine blood**.

Yet [[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor's]] descendants are not automatically gifted artists or prophets. His blessing is not mastery. It is **possibility**. They are expected to nurture creativity, protect artists and scholars, and ensure that imagination is never crushed simply because an idea seems strange or impossible. A ruler who forbids creativity or fears ideas merely because they challenge tradition would be turning away from [[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor's]] gift. Every great work begins with someone willing to imagine what has never existed.

# Lysandor's Sacred Animal

[[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor's]] sacred animal is the **house cat**, symbolizing **curiosity, imagination, independence, and quiet wonder**. Its playful nature and tendency to wander wherever curiosity leads reflect [[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor's]] belief that inspiration often comes when we are willing to explore the unexpected.

To his followers, the house cat represents the simple truth that wonder can be found in the smallest things.

# Lysandor's Aurekyn

The [[11. The Dreamveil, Aurekyn of Lysandor|Dreamveil]] is the divine embodiment of **dreams, imagination, inspiration, and wonder**. Created by [[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor]] from unspoken stories, unfinished melodies, and impossible dreams, it carries the seeds of future creations upon its wings.

It is an ethereal feline Aurekyn with soft ivory fur and immense translucent moth-like wings, accompanied by long ribbon-like antennae and luminous opalescent eyes. As it moves, shimmering dream motes drift from its wings, briefly becoming images of impossible wonders before fading into light. The [[11. The Dreamveil, Aurekyn of Lysandor|Dreamveil]] does not create inspiration for mortals, it merely awakens the inspirational spark within them already.
