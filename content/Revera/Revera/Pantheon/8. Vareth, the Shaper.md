[[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth]] is the goddess of **craftsmanship, creation, and transformation**. She teaches that the world is not merely something to inhabit, but something mortals can shape with their own hands. To [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth]], true craftsmanship is more than making something beautiful; it is the patience, skill, and purpose that transforms raw material into something meaningful.

---

# Her Place Among the Twelve

The gods of Revera aren't divided into conventional good and evil. They embody fundamental aspects of existence, and they don't wage divine wars against one another.

[[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth]] belongs to **The Pillars of Civilization**, alongside:

- [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon, the Arbiter]]
- [[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim, the Archivist]]

This triad represents the foundations upon which mortal civilization is built.

A simple way of understanding the three is:

- **Rheon establishes the laws that hold civilization together.**
- **Thalorim preserves the knowledge that allows it to grow.**
- **Vareth gives mortals the skill to build it.**

Together, they represent **law, knowledge, and creation**.

# Vareth and Creation
![[Symbol Vareth.jpeg|right|600]]
[[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth]] is the **Shaper**, goddess of craftsmanship, creation, and transformation. Her domains encompass strength, endurance, craftsmanship, and creation

[[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth]] teaches that creation is an act of patience. Stone does not become a fortress in a single day. Metal does not become a sword simply because someone desires it. Wood must be shaped, clay fired, and tools mastered.

For [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth]], the value of a creation lies not only in what it becomes, but in the skill and dedication required to make it. She therefore values the craftsman as much as the finished work.

---

# Vareth's First Descent

[[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth's]] first descent into the mortal world occurred in **Cael'vareth**. When she descended, she found the land raw and unfinished, and began shaping the stone around her with her own hands. The marks of her craftsmanship remain upon the stone to this day.

These divine marks became the **Sacred Remnant of her Descent**, preserved as a reminder that even the world itself was once shaped from something formless. Cael'vareth eventually became a center of craftsmanship, stonework, engineering, and construction, attracting artisans and builders who seek to master their trades beneath [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth's]] watch.

---

# The Bloodline of Cael'vareth

During her descent, [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth]] blessed a mortal who became the first ancestor of the ruling bloodline of Cael'vareth. That lineage continues into the present day, meaning its rulers carry **[[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth's]] divine blood**.

But [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth's]] blessing does not make her descendants master craftsmen. Her blood gives them responsibility, not mastery. A ruler who surrounds themselves with grand monuments but neglects the workers who build them would be acting against [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth's]] teachings.

To [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth]], the worth of a creation is found not merely in its grandeur, but in the hands and hearts that shaped it. A ruler who wishes to honor her blood must understand the value of every craftsman, from the humble mason to the greatest architect. A masterpiece begins with the hands that shape it.

---

# Vareth's Sacred Animal

[[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth's]] sacred animal is the **tortoise**, symbolizing **patience, endurance, stability, and longevity**. Its enduring nature reflects [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth's]] belief that great works are not created quickly, but through dedication, careful craftsmanship, and the strength to endure.

To her followers, the tortoise represents the wisdom that what is built to last must be given time to take shape.

# Vareth's Aurekyn

The [[8. The Worldhide, Aurekyn of Vareth|Worldhide]] is the divine embodiment of **strength, endurance, craftsmanship, and creation**. Created by [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth]] to shape and protect the physical world, it carries the ancient power of the earth within its enormous form.

Its colossal body resembles a living mountain, covered in layered stone, metal, and crystal. Ancient runes glow across its hide, while massive horns and mineral growths rise from its back like natural fortifications. Where the [[8. The Worldhide, Aurekyn of Vareth|Worldhide]] walks, the earth remembers her touch. Stone shifts, mountains settle, and new foundations are born.
