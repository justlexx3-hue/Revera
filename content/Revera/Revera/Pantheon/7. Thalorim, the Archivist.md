[[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim]] is the god of **knowledge, wisdom, and truth**. He watches over every discovery, library, academy, and archive, encouraging mortals to question what they know rather than simply accepting what they are told. To [[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim]], knowledge is not about possessing every answer, but having the **curiosity to seek them**.

---

# His Place Among the Twelve

The gods of Revera aren't divided into conventional good and evil. They embody fundamental aspects of existence, and they don't wage divine wars against one another.

[[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim]] belongs to **The Pillars of Civilization**, alongside:

- [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon, the Arbiter]]
- [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth, the Shaper]]

This triad represents the foundations upon which mortal civilization is built.

A simple way of understanding the three is:

- **Rheon establishes the laws that hold civilization together.**
- **Thalorim preserves the knowledge that allows it to grow.**
- **Vareth gives mortals the skill to build it.**

Together, they represent **law, knowledge, and creation**.

# Thalorim and Knowledge
![[Symbol Thalorim.jpeg|right|600]]
[[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim]] is the **Archivist**, and his domain encompasses Knowledge, Wisdom, Truth, History, Discovery and Memory. Every discovery belongs to [[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim]]. Every library, academy, and archive stands beneath his watchful eye.

But [[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim]] does not believe that knowledge is valuable simply because it is old or difficult to obtain. He values curiosity above certainty. A person who believes they already know everything has no reason to keep learning. To [[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim]], wisdom comes from understanding that there will **always be another question**.

# Thalorim's First Descent

[[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim's]] first descent into the mortal world occurred in the [[Thalor Peaks]]. There, he spoke his first words to mortals, and those words were preserved by the people who witnessed his arrival. His first words remain **carved into the oldest archive of the Thalor Peaks**, becoming the sacred remnant of his descent. The site eventually became a center of scholarship and preservation, attracting historians, scribes, scholars, and seekers of forgotten knowledge from across Revera.

The words themselves are treated with enormous reverence. Not because they contain every answer. But because they represent the moment when **divine knowledge first entered mortal history**.

---

# The Bloodline of the Thalor Peaks

During his descent, [[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim]] blessed a mortal who became the first ancestor of the ruling bloodline of the [[Thalor Peaks]]. That lineage continues into the present day, meaning the rulers of the region carry **[[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim's]] divine blood**.

Yet his descendants are not considered inherently wiser than anyone else. In fact, the burden of his bloodline is the responsibility to remain willing to learn. A ruler who suppresses knowledge, destroys history, or refuses to question their own beliefs would be betraying [[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim's]] teachings. His descendants are expected to preserve knowledge for future generations rather than hoard it for themselves.

# Thalorim's Sacred Animal

[[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim's]] sacred animal is the **owl**, representing wisdom, patience, observation, and the pursuit of knowledge.

Owls are believed to be mortal reflections of the [[7. The Echomantle, Aurekyn of Thalorim|Echomantle's]] quiet vigilance, watching the world from the darkness while others sleep.

# Thalorim's Aurekyn

The [[7. The Echomantle, Aurekyn of Thalorim|Echomantle]] is the divine embodiment of **memory, wisdom, and preserved knowledge**. Created by [[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim]] to ensure that no truth worthy of remembrance is ever truly lost, it wanders the hidden libraries and forgotten places of Revera.

Its luminous ivory feathers resemble translucent pages covered in shifting ancient writing, while its immense wings unfold into **The Living Archive**, filled with forgotten languages, celestial maps, and lost histories. A crown of crystalline sapphire Quills rises behind its head, while its piercing sapphire eyes reflect the wisdom of countless ages.

The [[7. The Echomantle, Aurekyn of Thalorim|Echomantle]] does not simply remember history.

**It carries history with it.**
