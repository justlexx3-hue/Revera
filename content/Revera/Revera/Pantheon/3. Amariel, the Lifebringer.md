[[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel]], the Lifebringer, is the divine embodiment of **life, growth, and renewal**. Where [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion]] gave the world its first hope, [[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel]] filled it with life.  Nurturing the forests, rivers, seasons, and all living things. To her, life is not defined by permanence, but by its endless ability to **grow, endure, and begin anew**.

------
# Her Place Among the Twelve

The gods of Revera aren't divided into conventional good and evil. They embody fundamental aspects of existence, and they don't wage divine wars against one another.

Amariel belongs to **The Cycle of Existence**, alongside:

- [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion, the Prime Light]]
- [[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel, the Sheperd]]

This makes her relationship with Elarion and Eryndrel particularly important.

A simple way of understanding the three is:

- **Elarion gives hope.**
- **Amariel gives life.**
- **Eryndrel guides what comes after life.**

Amariel is the living heart of this cycle. Where Elarion brought the first light and Eryndrel watches over the final passage, Amariel governs everything that grows, flourishes, withers, and begins anew.

# Amariel and Life
![[Symbol Amariel.jpeg|right|600]]
Amariel is the **Lifebringer**, the divine embodiment of nature, growth, and renewal. She governs: Forests, Seasons, Growth, Fertility, Renewal and the natural cycle of life. Farmers, druids, healers, and rangers all offer prayers in her name, asking not simply for abundance, but for the continued health of the living world.

Amariel does not command nature as a ruler commands subjects. She **nurtures it**. Life grows according to its own nature, and Amariel's role is to ensure that life is given the opportunity to endure. Her philosophy is therefore not one of permanence. It is **renewal**. A fallen tree becomes nourishment for new growth. A dead field becomes fertile again. Winter gives way to spring. Such is the way of life.

# Amariel's First Descent

Amariel's first descent into the mortal world occurred in [[Ama'thul]]. The place became sacred as the site where the Lifebringer first walked among mortals, and its continued connection to her is marked by the **first tree she planted there**, which still grows to this day.

Unlike a monument carved in stone, [[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel's]] sacred remnant remains alive. Its continued growth is considered one of the clearest signs that her blessing has never truly left [[Ama'thul]].

---

# The Bloodline of Ama'thul

During her descent into [[Ama'thul]], [[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel]] blessed a mortal who became the first ancestor of the settlement's ruling bloodline. That lineage continues into the present day, meaning the rulers of [[Ama'thul]] carry **[[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel's]] divine blood**.

Yet [[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel's]] blessing does not make her descendants masters of nature. Quite the opposite. To rule under the blood of the Lifebringer is to carry a responsibility toward the living world. A ruler who allows the land to be poisoned, forests destroyed without thought, or life to be treated as nothing more than a resource would stand against the very principles inherited from [[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel]]. Her blood may run through the royal line. But the land decides whether they are worthy of it.

# Amariel's Sacred Animal
[[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel's]] sacred animal is the **doe**, which are believed to carry echoes of the [[3. The Bloomheart, Aurekyn of Amariel|Bloomheart]]. The doe represents the gentler aspects of nature: nurturing, protection, fertility, grace, and the continuation of life. Yet, it is also a creature of instinct and survival, capable of enduring harsh seasons and protecting its young when threatened. This reflects [[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel's]] understanding of nature.

# Amariel's Aurekyn

The [[3. The Bloomheart, Aurekyn of Amariel|Bloomheart]] is the divine embodiment of **life, growth, and renewal**, created by [[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel]] when the first seeds took root in the newborn world. Wherever it wanders, forests awaken, flowers bloom, and life flourishes.

The [[3. The Bloomheart, Aurekyn of Amariel|Bloomheart]] is a majestic four-legged creature with an elegant, graceful form. Its body is woven from **smooth living wood**, covered in moss, flowering vines, emerald leaves and seasonal growth A crown of flowering branches rises from its head, alive with blossoms, fruit, and even nesting birds. Butterflies, bees, and woodland creatures are naturally drawn to it, while its emerald eyes shine with the warmth of spring itself.
