She governs the bonds that bring people together, believing that even the greatest achievements mean little if there is no one with whom to share them. To [[9. # Her Place Among the Twelve

The gods of Revera aren't divided into conventional good and evil. They embody fundamental aspects of existence, and they don't wage divine wars against one another.

[[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra]] belongs to **The Tapestry of Mortals**, alongside:

- [[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana, the Threadkeeper]]
- [[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos, the Masked]]

This triad represents the unseen forces that shape mortal lives.

A simple way of understanding the three is:

- **Lyriana weaves the paths available to mortals.**
- **Calendra binds the people who walk those paths together.**
- **Kaelos shapes the identities they discover along the way.**

Together, they represent **fate, connection, and transformation**.

# Calendra and Connection
![[Symbol Calandra.jpeg|right|600]]
[[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra]] is the **Bondweaver**, goddess of the bonds that unite people. Her domains encompass love, family, friendship, community, and home. She believes that no mortal life exists in isolation. Every friendship, family, promise, and shared home adds another thread to the great tapestry of existence.

[[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra]] does not create love where none exists. Instead, she strengthens the bonds that are already there, encouraging trust, loyalty, reconciliation, and companionship. Her teachings remind mortals that relationships require care. A bond can be strengthened through kindness and devotion, but it can also be weakened through neglect or betrayal. Connection is a gift, but keeping it alive is a choice.

# Calendra's First Descent

[[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra's]] first descent into the mortal world occurred in [[Can'Dorel]]. There, she kindled her first hearth, and its flame has **never gone cold**. The hearth became the sacred remnant of her descent and one of the most revered symbols of [[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra's]] presence.

[[Can'Dorel]] grew into a place associated with family, hospitality, community, and the sanctity of the home, with travelers and pilgrims welcomed beneath the warmth of [[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra's]] eternal flame.

---

# The Bloodline of Can'dorel

During her descent, [[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra]] blessed a mortal who became the first ancestor of the ruling bloodline of [[Can'Dorel]]. That lineage continues into the present day, meaning its rulers carry **[[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra's]] divine blood**.

Yet [[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra's]] blessing does not make her descendants automatically beloved or trusted. Her bloodline carries a responsibility to bring people together rather than divide them. A ruler who creates needless division, breaks sacred promises, or places their own ambition above the wellbeing of their people may carry [[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra's]] blood, but they have abandoned her teachings. Her descendants are expected to understand that a kingdom is not merely land and walls.

# Calendra's Sacred Animal

[[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra's]] sacred animal is the **Borzoi**, symbolizing loyalty, companionship, devotion, and the quiet comfort of home. Its gentle nature and deep bond with those it trusts reflect [[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra's]] belief that love is built through patience, trust, and unwavering companionship.

To her followers, the Borzoi represents the simple truth that **no one should have to walk through life alone**.

# Calendra's Aurekyn

The [[9. The Concordance, Aurekyn of Calendra|Concordance]] is the divine embodiment of **companionship, trust, unity, and devotion**. Fashioned by [[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra]] at the dawn of the world, it quietly strengthens the bonds between those who cross its path.

Its elegant body is woven from ivory and crimson living strands, with a radiant Halo Weave floating behind its head. Its warm amber eyes carry quiet understanding, meeting every gaze with kindness rather than judgment. The [[9. The Concordance, Aurekyn of Calendra|Concordance]] cannot create love or loyalty where none exists, it simply reminds mortals of the bonds they already share.
