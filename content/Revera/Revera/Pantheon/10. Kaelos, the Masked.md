[[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos]] is the god of **change, trickery, and transformation**. He is the patron of those who reinvent themselves, reminding mortals that identity is never fixed and that every person may become something different from what they once were. To [[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos]], a mask is not always a lie; sometimes it is the face we need while discovering who we truly are.

# His Place Among the Twelve

The gods of Revera aren't divided into conventional good and evil. They embody fundamental aspects of existence, and they don't wage divine wars against one another.

[[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos]] belongs to **The Tapestry of Mortals**, alongside:

- [[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana, the Threadkeeper]]
- [[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra, the Bondweaver]]

This triad represents the unseen forces that shape mortal lives.

A simple way of understanding the three is:

- **Lyriana weaves the paths available to mortals.**
- **Calendra binds the people who walk those paths together.**
- **Kaelos allows them to change who they are along the way.**

Together, they represent **fate, connection, identity, and transformation**.

# Kaelos and Transformation
![[Symbol Kaelos.jpeg|right|600]]
[[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos]] governs **change, identity, trickery, and transformation**. He is the patron of performers, rogues, wanderers and others who live between identities. [[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos]] understands that a mortal may wear many faces throughout a lifetime: child and elder, student and teacher, ruler and servant, hero and exile.

He does not condemn those who wear masks. Instead, he asks whether the wearer understands why they are wearing one. A mask can protect. A mask can deceive. A mask can give someone the courage to become who they were always capable of being.

But eventually, every mortal must decide whether the face beneath the mask is one they are willing to accept.

# Kaelos's First Descent

[[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos']] first descent into the mortal world occurred in [[Kaelindor]]. His descent site remains an unusual sacred place, for it **subtly changes depending upon who observes it**. No two people are entirely certain they have seen the same place. Paths shift. Details appear different. Familiar landmarks seem subtly altered.

To [[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos']] followers, this is not an illusion to be solved. It is a reminder that truth can look different depending upon the eyes through which it is perceived.

---

# The Bloodline of Kaelindor

During his descent, [[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos]] blessed a mortal who became the first ancestor of the ruling bloodline of [[Kaelindor]]. That lineage continues into the present day, meaning its rulers carry **[[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos's]] divine blood**.

Yet his descendants are not granted a fixed identity or destiny. Quite the opposite. They inherit the responsibility to recognize that people and societies must be allowed to change. A ruler who clings desperately to an identity that no longer serves their people, or uses tradition as an excuse to prevent necessary change, would be acting against [[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos']] teachings.

His bloodline is not a promise of who they will become. It is a reminder that they can become something else.

---

# Kaelos's Sacred Animal

[[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos']] sacred animal is the **fox**, symbolizing cleverness, adaptability, mystery, and the ability to move between worlds unseen.

To his followers, the fox represents the freedom to change one's path and the wisdom to never reveal more of oneself than necessary.

# Kaelos's Aurekyn

The [[10. The Shadeslip, Aurekyn of Kaelos|Shadeslip]] is the divine embodiment of **mystery, identity, hidden truths, and transformation**. Created by [[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos]] from the fragments of every identity yet to be lived, it wanders twilight forests and forgotten crossroads where certainty begins to fade.

Its sleek obsidian body has graceful, fox-like proportions, luminous violet eyes, and a flowing **Veilmane** of living shadow filled with drifting ceremonial masks. Behind it hangs an incomplete halo of fractured silver arcs known as the **Veil Crown**. The [[10. The Shadeslip, Aurekyn of Kaelos|Shadeslip]] does not expose people for what they pretend to be but help them discover who they truly are.
