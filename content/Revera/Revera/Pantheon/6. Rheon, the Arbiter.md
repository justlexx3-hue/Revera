[[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon]] is the god of **order, law, justice, and balance**. He teaches that civilization survives not through power alone, but through the willingness of those who hold power to use it fairly. To [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon]], true authority is earned through wisdom, integrity, and service to others.

---

# His Place Among the Twelve

The gods of Revera aren't divided into conventional good and evil. They embody fundamental aspects of existence, and they don't wage divine wars against one another.

[[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon]] belongs to **The Pillars of Civilization**, alongside:

- [[7. Thalorim, the Archivist|Thalorim, the Archivist]]
- [[8. Vareth, the Shaper|Vareth, the Shaper]]

This triad represents the foundations upon which mortal civilization is built.

A simple way of understanding the three is:

- **Rheon establishes the laws that hold civilization together.**
- **Thalorim preserves the knowledge that allows it to grow.**
- **Vareth gives mortals the skill to build it.**

Together, they represent the structures, knowledge, and craftsmanship that allow kingdoms and societies to endure.

# Rheon and Justice
![[Symbol Rheon.jpeg|right|600]]
[[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon]] embodies **justice without cruelty and order without tyranny**. Kings invoke him before judgments, while paladins swear their oaths beneath his name. To [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon]], law exists to protect civilization, not to give rulers unquestioned authority. A law that serves only those who created it is not justice.

Likewise, order without compassion can become tyranny, while compassion without order can allow injustice to flourish. [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon]] therefore seeks **balance** between the two. His greatest lesson is that those who hold authority must be willing to place themselves beneath the same principles they expect others to follow.

# Rheon's First Descent

[[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon's]] first descent into the mortal world occurred in [[Highcourt]]. When he descended, the place where his feet first touched the earth became marked by a great **landing stone**, which remains beneath the city to this day. As Highcourt grew into a center of law and governance, the stone became a sacred reminder of the foundation upon which civilization must stand.

Though the city has grown around and above it, the stone remains untouched. It is said that every ruler who sits upon [[Highcourt|Highcourt's]] throne ultimately rests above the place where [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon]] first stood.

---

# The Bloodline of Highcourt

During his descent into [[Highcourt]], [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon]] blessed a mortal who became the first ancestor of the settlement's ruling bloodline. That lineage continues into the present day, meaning the rulers of [[Highcourt]] carry **[[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon's]] divine blood**.

But [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon's]] teachings make one thing particularly clear: **Divine blood does not make a ruler just.** His descendants inherit the responsibility of leadership, not an automatic claim to righteousness.

A ruler who abuses their authority, places themselves above the law, or governs through fear may carry [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon's]] blood, but they have abandoned his teachings. For [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon]], the worth of a ruler is measured not by the power they possess, but by **what they are willing to sacrifice for those beneath their protection**.

---

# Rheon's Sacred Animal

[[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon's]] sacred animal is the **lion**, representing strength, courage, authority, and noble leadership. To his followers, the lion is not a symbol of domination, but of **responsible power**. A true ruler should possess the strength to command, yet the wisdom to know when command is not enough.

# Rheon's Aurekyn

The [[6. The Crownmane, Aurekyn of Rheon|Crownmane]] is the divine embodiment of **justice, honor, courage, and rightful rule**. Created by [[6. Rheon, the Arbiter|Rheon]] to remind rulers that true authority is earned through wisdom and integrity, it appears when the balance of justice is at stake. Its powerful body is covered in radiant bronze fur, crowned by a magnificent mane of **living golden light and crystalline fragments**. Its impossibly calm amber eyes see beyond titles and wealth, judging only the character within.
