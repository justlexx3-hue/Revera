# Her Place Among the Twelve

The gods of Revera aren't divided into conventional good and evil. They embody fundamental aspects of existence, and they don't wage divine wars against one another.

[[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana]] belongs to **The Tapestry of Mortals**, alongside:

- [[9. Calendra, the Bondweaver|Calendra, the Bondweaver]]
- [[10. Kaelos, the Masked|Kaelos, the Masked]]

This triad represents the unseen forces that shape the lives, relationships, and identities of mortals.

A simple way of understanding the three is:

- **Lyriana weaves the paths available to mortals.**
- **Calendra binds the lives that cross those paths.**
- **Kaelos reminds mortals that they can change who they are along the way.**

Together, they represent the countless threads that make up a mortal life.

# Lyriana and Fate
![[Symbol Lyriana.jpeg|right|600]]
[[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana]] is the **Threadkeeper**, goddess of **Fate, Destiny, and Possibility**. She weaves the great tapestry of every mortal life, but she does not dictate every choice. Instead, she creates the threads and possibilities available to each soul, leaving mortals free to decide which path they will walk. To [[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana]], fate is not a predetermined road. It is a **tapestry of possibilities**.

Every choice creates another thread. Every decision may open one path while closing another. What appears inevitable from one perspective may simply be one possibility among countless others. This is why [[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana]] values **choice over certainty**. The future is not something mortals are simply waiting to discover, it is something they help weave.

# Lyriana's First Descent

[[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana's]] first descent into the mortal world occurred in [[Kald Norn]]. When she descended, the stars above the settlement appeared unusually clear, revealing a sky unlike any the people had ever seen before.

That clarity became the **Sacred Remnant of her Descent**. Even generations later, the stars above [[Kald Norn]] seem brighter and more numerous than elsewhere in Revera, and the people of the settlement believe that [[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana's]] first threads still stretch across the heavens. [[Kald Norn]] consequently became a place of **divination, prophecy, and contemplation**, where those seeking to understand the paths before them look to the stars.

---

# The Bloodline of Kald Norn

During her descent into [[Kald Norn]], [[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana]] blessed a mortal who became the first ancestor of the settlement's ruling bloodline. That lineage continues into the present day, meaning the rulers of [[Kald Norn]] carry **[[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana's]] divine blood**.

Yet [[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana's]] blessing does not grant her descendants knowledge of their own destiny it carries the responsibility of understanding that **no ruler can know every thread**. A ruler who attempts to force destiny upon their people would be acting against [[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana's]] teachings. Her descendants are meant to guide, interpret, and understand the possibilities before them, while remembering that every mortal must ultimately choose their own path.

---

# Lyriana's Sacred Animal

[[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana's]] sacred animal is the **silk moth**, whose delicate wings and ability to spin silk reflect the fragile threads of fate. Its transformation from caterpillar to moth represents change, possibility, and the many forms a life may take. To her followers, the silk moth is a symbol of **destiny, transformation, dreams, and the unseen threads connecting all lives**.

# Lyriana's Aurekyn

The **Fatespinner** is the divine embodiment of **fate, time, destiny, and possibility**. Created by [[5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper|Lyriana]] before the first mortal drew breath, it silently weaves the invisible Threads of Fate that bind every life and every future.

Its elegant body is formed from **ivory, living crystal, and polished wood**, crowned with branching Loomhorns that spin luminous threads into the air. A vast mantle of living moon-silk spreads behind it like an ever-changing web, while six softly glowing opal eyes reflect moments yet to come. The Fatespinner never dictates destiny, it merely spins it for mortals to choose.
