# The Twelve of Revera

Unlike many worlds, the gods of Revera are not divided into good and evil. They embody the fundamental aspects of existence itself. They inspire, guide, and bless, but they do not wage war against one another.

The true corruption of Revera comes from forces **outside** the divine order.

---

# The Four Divine Triads

### The Cycle of Existence

The eternal cycle through which all living things pass.

- Elarion, the First Light
- Amariel, the Lifebringer
- Eryndrel, the Sheperd
---
### The Pillars of Civilization

The gods who shaped kingdoms, knowledge, law and progress.

- Rheon, the Arbiter
- Thalorim, the Archivist
- Vareth, the Shaper
---
### The Tapestry of Mortals

The unseen forces that shape every mortal life.

- Lyriana, the Threadkeeper
- Calendra, the Bondweaver
- Kaelos, the Masked
---
### The Endless Horizon

Those who call mortals beyond the familiar.

- Syrethis, the Tidebearer
- Lysandor, the Inspirer
- Elira, the Wayfarer
---

# 1. Elarion, the First Light

**Divine Triad**  
The Cycle of Existence

**Domain**  
Light • Hope • Unity • Divine Creation

**Symbol**  
Lantern

**Sacred Colors**  
Gold & Ivory

**Sacred Animal**  
Stag

The eldest of the Twelve and the first divine presence to bless a mortal. Elarion embodies hope, compassion, and the belief that mortals should forge their own destiny. His bloodline continues through the rulers of Luminae, though he has remained silent for centuries, trusting the world to choose its own future.

---
# 2. Eryndrel, the Shadowed Guide

**Divine Triad**  
The Cycle of Existence

**Domain**  
Death • Passage • The Afterlife

**Symbol**  
Hourglass

**Sacred Colors**  
Black & Silver

**Sacred Animal**  
Raven

The gentle shepherd of souls. Eryndrel is not feared by those who understand him, for death is simply another journey. His first appearance was in Duskport, making it one of the holiest places for funerary rites and remembrance.

---
# 3. Amariel, the Lifebringer

**Divine Triad**  
The Cycle of Existence

**Domain**  
Nature • Growth • Renewal

**Symbol**  
Spiral Wreath of Leaves

**Sacred Colors**  
Emerald & Amber

**Sacred Animal**  
Doe

Where Elarion gave hope, Amariel gave life. She governs forests, rivers, seasons, and the endless cycle of renewal. Farmers, druids, healers, and rangers all offer prayers in her name.

---
# 4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer

**Divine Triad**  
The Endless Horizon

**Domain**  
Sea • Storms • Tides

**Symbol**  
Three-Spiraled Conch

**Sacred Colors**  
Deep Blue & Teal

**Sacred Animal**  
Whale

She is both calm seas and raging tempests. Sailors pray to her before every voyage, understanding that she offers no guarantees, only respect for those who understand the sea.

---
# 5. Lyriana, the Threadkeeper

**Divine Triad**  
The Tapestry of Mortals

**Domain**  
Fate • Destiny • Possibility

**Symbol**  
Woven Threads

**Sacred Colors**  
Violet & White

**Sacred Animal**  
Spider

Lyriana weaves the tapestry of every mortal life. She does not decide every choice, only the threads available to be woven. Mortals remain free to choose which path they walk.

---
# 6. Rheon, the Arbiter

**Divine Triad**  
The Pillars of Civilization

**Domain**  
Order • Law • Justice • Balance

**Symbol**  
Sword

**Sacred Colors**  
Crimson & Steel

**Sacred Animal**  
Lion

Rheon embodies justice without cruelty and order without tyranny. Kings invoke him before judgments, and paladins swear their oaths beneath his name.

---
# 7. Thalorim, the Archivist

**Divine Triad**  
The Pillars of Civilization

**Domain**  
Knowledge • Wisdom • Truth

**Symbol**  
Quill

**Sacred Colors**  
Indigo & Bronze

**Sacred Animal**  
Owl

Every discovery belongs to Thalorim. Every library, academy, and archive stands beneath his watchful eye. He values curiosity above certainty.

---
# 8. Vareth, the Shaper

**Divine Triad**  
The Pillars of Civilization

**Domain**  
Craft • Invention • Creation

**Symbol**  
Anvil

**Sacred Colors**  
Copper & Charcoal

**Sacred Animal**  
Bear

The patron of blacksmiths, architects, inventors, artists, and artisans. Vareth teaches that civilization is built not by miracles, but by skilled hands.

---
# 9. Calendra, the Bondweaver

**Divine Triad**  
The Tapestry of Mortals

**Domain**  
Love • Family • Friendship • Community • Home

**Symbol**  
Intertwined Hands

**Sacred Colors**  
Rose & Cream

**Sacred Animal**  
Dove

Calendra governs every bond between people. Romantic love, lifelong friendship, family, community, and the warmth of home all fall beneath her embrace.

---
# 10. Kaelos, the Masked

**Divine Triad**  
The Tapestry of Mortals

**Domain**  
Change • Trickery • Transformation

**Symbol**  
Shifting Mask

**Sacred Colors**  
Purple & Black

**Sacred Animal**  
Fox

The patron of actors, rogues, revolutionaries, and those who reinvent themselves. Kaelos reminds mortals that change is inevitable and identity is never fixed.

---
# 11. Lysandor, the Inspirer

**Divine Triad**  
The Endless Horizon

**Domain**  
Dreams • Inspiration • Imagination

**Symbol**  
Crescent Moon

**Sacred Colors**  
Midnight Blue & White

**Sacred Animal**  
Moth

Artists, musicians, storytellers, inventors, and prophets all claim to hear Lysandor's whispers while they dream. Every masterpiece begins as one of his quiet gifts.

---
# 12. Elira, the Wayfarer

**Divine Triad**  
The Endless Horizon

**Domain**  
Exploration • Discovery • Adventure

**Symbol**  
Compass

**Sacred Colors**  
Turquoise & Sand

**Sacred Animal**  
Swallow

The youngest of the Twelve. Elira blesses those who venture into the unknown, whether across oceans, through forgotten ruins, or into unexplored parts of themselves.
