# Her Place Among the Twelve

The gods of Revera aren't divided into conventional good and evil. They embody fundamental aspects of existence, and they don't wage divine wars against one another.

[[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis]] belongs to **The Endless Horizon**, alongside:

- [[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor, the Inspirer]]
- [[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira, the Wayfarer]]

This triad represents the forces that draw mortals beyond what they already know.

A simple way of understanding the three is:

- **Syrethis calls mortals toward the unknown.**
- **Lysandor inspires them to imagine what might be found there.**
- **Elira gives them the courage to go and discover it.**

[[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis]] is therefore not simply a goddess of the sea. The ocean is the greatest expression of her domain because it represents **the vast, powerful, and unknowable world beyond the familiar**.

# Syrethis and the Endless Sea
![[Symbol Syrethis.jpeg|right|600]]
[[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis]] is the **Tidebearer**, goddess of the sea, storms, and tides. She embodies both the **calm sea and the raging tempest**. Sailors pray to her before every voyage, but they understand that she offers no guarantees. The sea demands respect from all who venture upon it. Her domain encompasses the oceans, tides, storms, maritime travel and unpredictability.

[[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis]] does not seek to tame the ocean. She represents its nature. The sea can carry a sailor safely across half the world or swallow a ship without warning. Neither is an act of cruelty. Both are simply part of the sea's nature. To follow [[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis]] is therefore to understand that **not everything can be controlled**.


---

# Syrethis's First Descent

[[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis's]] first descent into the mortal world occurred at [[Cape Syren]]. There, her arrival forever changed the waters surrounding the settlement. The waters of [[Cape Syren]] **never completely cease moving**. 

Even in the calmest weather, the sea around the site continues to shift and turn, as though some trace of [[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis's]] presence remains beneath the surface. The phenomenon became known as the **Tidebearer's Remnant**, and [[Cape Syren]] grew into a sacred place for sailors, navigators, and those preparing to venture beyond familiar shores.

---

# The Bloodline of Cape Syren

During her descent, [[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis]] blessed a mortal who became the first ancestor of the **ruling bloodline of [[Cape Syren]]**. That lineage continues into the present day, meaning the rulers of [[Cape Syren]] carry the divine blood of the Tidebearer.

But [[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis's]] blessing was never a promise of mastery over the sea. Quite the opposite. Her descendants are expected to understand that the sea cannot be commanded simply because one carries the blood of its goddess. A ruler who respects the ocean, protects those who depend upon it, and understands the dangers of venturing beyond the familiar honors [[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis]].

---

# Syrethis's Sacred Animal
[[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis's]] sacred animal is the **sea serpent**, a creature that embodies the ancient power, mystery, and unpredictability of the ocean. Its winding form reflects the endless movement of currents and tides, while its ability to disappear beneath the waves represents the depths of the sea that remain beyond mortal understanding.

To sailors, the sea serpent is both a symbol of **respect and caution**, reminding them that the ocean cannot be mastered, only navigated. It is said that the greatest sea serpents are distant mortal echoes of the **Deepwake's ancient nature**.

# Syrethis's Aurekyn

The Deepwake is the divine embodiment of **the oceans, tides, and eternal flow of the sea**. [[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis]] created it when the first waters embraced the shores of Revera, entrusting it with maintaining harmony throughout the seas.

The Deepwake is a colossal marine Aurekyn of impossible scale. Its sapphire-blue hide shimmers with silver beneath the waves, while **coral reefs, glowing anemones, and pearl-like shells** naturally flourish along its back. Its enormous turquoise eyes shine like lanterns in the abyss, and every movement leaves glowing currents drifting through the water.
