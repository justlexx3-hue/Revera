[[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira]] is the goddess of **travel, freedom, exploration, and discovery**. She calls mortals beyond the boundaries of the familiar, encouraging them to cross new horizons, follow unfamiliar roads, and discover what lies beyond what they already know. To [[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira]], the world is not meant to be merely observed from the safety of home, but **experienced through the journey**.

---

# Her Place Among the Twelve

The gods of Revera aren't divided into conventional good and evil. They embody fundamental aspects of existence, and they don't wage divine wars against one another.

[[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira]] belongs to **The Endless Horizon**, alongside:

- [[4. Syrethis, the Tidebearer|Syrethis, the Tidebearer]]
- [[11. Lysandor, the Inspirer|Lysandor, the Inspirer]]

This triad represents the forces that draw mortals beyond the boundaries of the familiar.

A simple way of understanding the three is:

- **Syrethis calls mortals toward the unknown.**
- **Lysandor inspires them to imagine what might be found there.**
- **Elira gives them the courage to go and discover it.**

Together, they represent **wonder, inspiration, exploration, and discovery**.

# Elira and the Journey
![[Symbol Elira.jpeg|right|600]]
[[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira]] governs travel, freedom, exploration, journey and adventure. She is the patron of travellers, sailors, explorers, merchants, pilgrims, and anyone who chooses to leave the familiar behind. To [[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira]], a journey is valuable even when it has no grand destination.

The road itself teaches. Every unfamiliar place offers something new to learn, and every journey changes the person who undertakes it. [[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira]] therefore encourages mortals not to fear the unknown, but to meet it with curiosity and courage.

---

# Elira's First Descent

[[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira's]] first descent into the mortal world occurred in [[Port Meridian]]. When she arrived, the winds around the settlement began to blow and **have never stopped**. These eternal winds became the Sacred Remnant of her descent. Even on otherwise still days, the air around [[Port Meridian]] continues to move, carrying the scent of distant places across the harbor.

The city grew into a major center of travel and exploration, with ships and travelers departing its shores in every direction. To the people of [[Port Meridian]], the endless wind is a reminder that there is always somewhere else to go.

---

# The Bloodline of Port Meridian

During her descent, [[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira]] blessed a mortal who became the first ancestor of the ruling bloodline of Port Meridian. That lineage continues into the present day, meaning its rulers carry **[[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira's]] divine blood**.

Yet her blessing does not make her descendants natural explorers. Instead, it gives them a responsibility to keep the road open for others. A ruler who closes their borders out of fear, prevents their people from traveling, or treats unfamiliar cultures with suspicion would be acting against [[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira's]] teachings.

Her descendants are expected to welcome travellers, protect the freedom to journey, and ensure that the paths beyond their borders remain open. A road is only meaningful if someone is free to walk it.

# Elira's Sacred Animal

[[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira's]] sacred animal is the **swallow**, symbolizing **freedom, travel, endurance, and the endless journey**. Its ability to cross vast distances and always return home reflects [[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira's]] belief that the world is meant to be explored, while reminding travelers that every journey has a place to return to.

To her followers, the swallow represents **the freedom to leave, the courage to wander, and the joy of finding one's way home**.

# Elira's Aurekyn

The [[12. The Skystrider, Aurekyn of Elira|Skystrider]] is the divine embodiment of **freedom, travel, exploration, and the endless horizon**. Created by [[12. Elira, the Wayfarer|Elira]] to guide those who venture beyond the familiar, it is said to appear wherever a new journey is about to begin.

Its sleek form resembles a swallow, with flowing wings and tail that trail into wisps of luminous cloud. Vast feathered wings carry it effortlessly across the skies, while its silver-blue feathers shimmers like moonlight on distant mountains. The [[12. The Skystrider, Aurekyn of Elira|Skystrider]] never remains in one place for long. Wherever it flies, the horizon calls.
