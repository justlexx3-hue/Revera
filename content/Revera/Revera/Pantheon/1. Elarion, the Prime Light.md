[[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion]] is the oldest of the Twelve and the **first divine presence to bless a mortal**. He represents the light that makes existence worth continuing rather than simply light as a physical phenomenon. His philosophy is fundamentally about **hope, compassion, unity, and mortal freedom**. He believes mortals are not meant to be puppets of the gods. They are meant to **forge their own destiny**.

-----
# His Place Among the Twelve

The gods of Revera aren't divided into conventional good and evil. They embody fundamental aspects of existence, and they don't wage divine wars against one another.

[[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion]] belongs to **The Cycle of Existence**, alongside:

- [[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel, the Sheperd]]
- [[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel, the Lifebringer]]


This makes his relationship with Amariel and Eryndrel particularly important.

A simple way of understanding the three is:

- **Elarion gives hope.**  
- **Amariel gives life.**  
- **Eryndrel guides what comes after life.**

# Elarion and Creation
![[Symbol Elarion.jpeg|right|600]]
[[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion]] is associated directly with **Divine Creation**, but there is an interesting distinction in Revera's theology. He isn't necessarily the god responsible for every physical thing that exists. Vareth, for example, governs creation through craft and the shaping of the world. Elarion represents the **first creative impulse**, the illumination that makes creation possible and gives it purpose. According to temple doctrine, [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion]] shaped the first [[1. The Starhart, Aurekyn of Elarion|Starhart]] from the roots of the first tree, the light of Astera, and a fallen star to spread his light across the mortal realms.

That makes the title **Prime Light** rather potent. Light came first conceptually. Before mortals could build, explore, love, remember, rule, dream, or even understand death, there had to be light. Many believe the stories regarding the **Prime Light** are what fuelled the creation of his symbol, the **Lantern**.

# Elarion's First Descent

[[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion's]] first descent into the mortal world occurred in [[Luminae]]. There, the Prime Light blessed the first mortal to receive divine favor, beginning the bloodline that would eventually become the ruling lineage of [[Luminae]]. His descent established [[Luminae]] as a place deeply associated with **hope, light, unity, and mortal freedom**.

The divine presence of [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion]] has never entirely left the city. Even after he withdrew from the mortal world, [[Luminae]] remained the heart of his worship and the seat of his bloodline.

-------

# Elarion's Silence

One of the most important pieces of existing lore: **[[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion]] has been silent for centuries.**

His bloodline continues through the rulers of [[Luminae]], but [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion]] himself has deliberately remained silent, trusting the world to choose its own future. This gives him a very different relationship with mortals from a conventional interventionist god. He isn't absent because he doesn't care. He is silent because he trusts the Reverans.

------
# The Bloodline of Luminae

[[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion's]] divine bloodline continues through the rulers of [[Luminae]]. That means the royal family of [[Luminae]] isn't merely claiming divine legitimacy as propaganda. There is an established theological basis for the claim.

However, [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion's]] philosophy creates an interesting tension here. His descendants possess divine blood. But [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion]] himself believes mortals should forge their own destiny. So divine ancestry does not automatically equal divine right. A ruler could descend from the **Prime Light** and still completely betray everything he represents.

--------

# Elarion's Sacred Animal
Elarion's sacred animal is the **stag**, symbolizing hope, wisdom, noble leadership, and renewal. Its strength and grace reflect Elarion's belief that mortals should walk their own paths with courage and compassion.

# Elarion's Aurekyn

The [[1. The Starhart, Aurekyn of Elarion|Starhart]] is the divine embodiment of **hope, guidance, and renewal**. Created by [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion]] from the roots of the first tree, the light of Astera, and a fallen star, it carries the First Light within its antlers.

Its ivory coat bears faint constellations, while its luminous crystal-and-wood antlers shine with golden light. Wherever it walks, darkness recedes and hope follows.
