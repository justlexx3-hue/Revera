[[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel]] is **the gentle shepherd of souls**. He was the second deity to appear for every beginning needs an end. His central philosophy is that death is not an ending, but **another journey**. He is not a god of suffering or punishment. For those who understand him, there is little reason to fear him. His first appearance was in [[Duskport]], which consequently became one of the holiest places in Revera for **funerary rites and remembrance**.

---------
# His Place Among the Twelve

The gods of Revera aren't divided into conventional good and evil. They embody fundamental aspects of existence, and they don't wage divine wars against one another.

[[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel]] belongs to **The Cycle of Existence**, alongside:

- [[1. Elarion, the Prime Light|Elarion, the Prime Light]]
- [[3. Amariel, the Lifebringer|Amariel, the Lifebringer]]

This makes his relationship with Amariel and Elarion particularly important.

A simple way of understanding the three is:

- **Elarion gives hope.**  
- **Amariel gives life.**  
- **Eryndrel guides what comes after life.**

# Eryndrel and Death
![[Symbol Eryndrel.jpeg|right|600]]
[[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel]] is the **gentle Shepherd of souls** and the guardian of the passage between life and the afterlife. When the first mortal died, [[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel]] saw that no path yet existed beyond life. From the **first night sky** and the **quiet between heartbeats**, he shaped the first [[2. The Gravewing, Aurekyn of Eryndrel|Gravewing]] to carry souls safely into the beyond. Since then, every peaceful passing is said to occur beneath its unseen wings.

[[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel]] does not judge the dead, nor does he punish them for the lives they lived. His role is simply to ensure that every soul reaches its destination. His symbol, the **Hourglass**, represents the inevitability of death. Eventually the final grain falls, just as every mortal life eventually reaches its end. Though when the glass is turned, the future becomes the past. To his followers, death is not an ending. It is **a crossing** to another journey

# Eryndrel's First Descent

[[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel's]] first descent into the mortal world occurred in [[Duskport]]. There, he appeared before the mortals and became the first divine presence to openly guide them through the mystery of death.

The place where he descended became sacred, and [[Duskport]] grew into one of the holiest places in Revera for **funerary rites, remembrance, and the peaceful passing of the dead**. Unlike many divine manifestations, [[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel's]] descent was not remembered for displays of power. It was remembered for **comfort**. He came to a people who had experienced death and taught them that they had not been abandoned.

---

# The Bloodline of Duskport

As with the other First Descents, [[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel]] blessed a mortal during his arrival in [[Duskport]]. That mortal became the first ancestor of the **Duskport ruling bloodline**, establishing a divine lineage that continues into the present day. The rulers of [[Duskport]] therefore carry the blood of the Shepherd himself.

Yet [[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel's]] blessing was never intended to make his descendants masters over death, nor does divine ancestry make a ruler worthy by itself. The blood may be inherited. **The Shepherd's virtues must still be earned.** A ruler who uses death as a weapon, desecrates the dead, or denies others a peaceful passage would stand in direct opposition to the deity whose blood runs through their veins.

# Eryndrel's Sacred Animal
[[2. Eryndrel, the Sheperd|Eryndrel's]] sacred animal is the **raven**. Ravens are believed to be mortal echoes of the [[2. The Gravewing, Aurekyn of Eryndrel|Gravewing]], carrying fragments of its watchful nature.

The [[2. The Gravewing, Aurekyn of Eryndrel|Gravewing]] is not an omen of death, but a promise that **no soul must walk its final journey alone**. Ravens are therefore treated with quiet respect throughout much of Revera, particularly around graveyards, battlefields, and places of mourning. A raven appearing during a funeral is generally regarded not as a warning, but as a sign that the departed is being watched over.

# Eryndrel's Aurekyn

The [[2. The Gravewing, Aurekyn of Eryndrel|Gravewing]] is the Aurekyn of Eryndrel, the Shepherd. It is a towering celestial bird with a wingspan exceeding **8 metres**, its black plumage revealing a sky filled with distant stars when viewed closely.

Its smooth, face bears softly glowing silver eyes that reflect **memories rather than light**. A mantle of pale feathers surrounds its neck like a ceremonial cloak, while its long tail feathers drift behind it like smoke. The [[2. The Gravewing, Aurekyn of Eryndrel|Gravewing]] is silent, patient, and compassionate. It simply remains beside those whose time has come, offering calm during their final moments.
